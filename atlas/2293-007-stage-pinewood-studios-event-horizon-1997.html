Filming location · Event Horizon (1997)

007 Stage, Pinewood Studios

The whole film was shot at Pinewood across seven stages from November 1996 to March 1997, with the gravity drive and engine room built on the enormous 007 Stage. Pinewood is a working studio, closed to the public.
