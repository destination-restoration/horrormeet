Givins Beverly Castle

Named among Illinois's reportedly haunted places. The Givins Beverly Castle (also known as The Irish Castle or The Castle) is a 19th-century building located at the corner of West 103rd Street and Longwood Avenue on the far Southwest Side of Chicago, Illinois.
