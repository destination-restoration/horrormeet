Filming location · A Nightmare on Elm Street 4: The Dream Master (1988)

Springwood High School / Venice High

Venice High School played Springwood High for The Dream Master, the campus where Sheila's asthma attack turns deadly in class. Its famous statue also fronted Rydell High in Grease.
