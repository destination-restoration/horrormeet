Filming location · Eyes Without a Face (1960)

Chateau du Haut du Bel Air, Le Chesnay

The surgeon's clinic, where the face transplants are carried out, was this chateau on the rue de Versailles. The building is barely changed but now sits inside the campus of a special education service, so it is not open to visitors.
