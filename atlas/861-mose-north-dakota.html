Mose, North Dakota

A ghost town in North Dakota. Mose is a ghost town in Griggs County, in the U.S. state of North Dakota. Founded in 1899, Mose is regularly known as "The Town That Blew Away" due to a large derecho which occurred on July 14, 1943.
