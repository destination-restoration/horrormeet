Filming location · REC (2007)

Casa Argelich, Rambla de Catalunya 34

The whole quarantined apartment block is this real Eixample building from 1896, including the entrance hall and the marble staircase. It has stood empty since 2004 and is used for film shoots, so there is nothing to visit inside.
