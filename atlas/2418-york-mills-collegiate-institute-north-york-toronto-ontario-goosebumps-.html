Filming location · Goosebumps (1995)

York Mills Collegiate Institute, North York, Toronto, Ontario

This building was used as the school in three episodes: Go Eat Worms, My Best Friend Is Invisible and Say Cheese and Die Again. It is a working public high school at 490 York Mills Road. It is not open to visitors, so view the exterior from the street.
