Filming location · Abigail (2024)

Glenmaroon House, Knockmaroon Hill

Almost all of the vampire ballerina film was shot inside this mansion on the edge of Phoenix Park, built for Guinness heir Arthur Ernest Guinness and later a convent and care home. The buildings are now vacant and PRIVATE, so view from the road.
