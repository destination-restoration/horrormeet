English Building (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign)

Named among Illinois's reportedly haunted places. The English Building, previously known as the Woman's Building and Bevier Hall, is a historic structure on the campus of the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. It is located on the west side of the Main Quad between Lincoln Hall and the Henry Administration Building.
