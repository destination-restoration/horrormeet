Monasterio de Santa Maria la Real de Valdeiglesias

The roofless Cistercian ruins at Pelayos de la Presa are the abbey where the blind Templar dead rise and ride. The monastery is owned by the town, has been partly consolidated and can be visited on scheduled tours.
