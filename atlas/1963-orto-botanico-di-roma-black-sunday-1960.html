Filming location · Black Sunday (1960)

Orto Botanico di Roma

The garden fountain scene between Princess Katia and her father was filmed in Rome's botanical garden on the slope of the Janiculum. The garden belongs to Sapienza University and is open to the public for an entrance fee.
