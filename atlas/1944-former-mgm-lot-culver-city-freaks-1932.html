Filming location · Freaks (1932)

Former MGM lot, Culver City

Tod Browning shot Freaks in 36 days on MGM's Culver City lot, reusing standing sets from a Garbo picture. The lot survives as a working studio in Culver City and is not open to the public, so it can only be seen from Washington Boulevard.
