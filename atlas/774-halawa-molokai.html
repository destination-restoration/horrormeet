Hālawa, Molokaʻi

A ghost town in Hawaii. Hālawa (Hawaiian: Hālawa, pronounced [haːˈlɐvə]) is a valley and ahupuaʻa (traditional land division) at the eastern end of the island of Molokaʻi in Hawaiʻi, United States. The valley extends some 2 miles inland from the sea.
