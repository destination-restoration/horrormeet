Franklin Castle

Named among Ohio's reportedly haunted places. Franklin Castle (also known as the Tiedemann House) is an American Victorian stone house, built in the American Queen Anne style, located at 4308 Franklin Boulevard in Cleveland's Ohio City neighborhood. The building has four stories and more than twenty rooms and eighty windows.
