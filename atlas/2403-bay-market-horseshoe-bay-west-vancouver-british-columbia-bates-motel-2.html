Filming location · Bates Motel (2013)

Bay Market, Horseshoe Bay, West Vancouver, British Columbia

For season 1 episode 7 the crew turned this corner grocery into Bay Market and Hardware, where Norman and Dylan buy supplies at a fall hardware sale and run into Bradley outside. The shop sits at 6414 Bruce Street on the corner with Royal Avenue. It is a business on a public street, so the storefront is easy to see from the sidewalk.
