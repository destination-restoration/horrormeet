Filming location · The X-Files (1993)

Riverview Hospital, Coquitlam

The closed 1913 psychiatric hospital supplied most of the show's hospital and asylum interiors from the pilot through season five, and it is one of the most filmed sites in British Columbia. The hospital shut in 1992 and the lands are being redeveloped.
