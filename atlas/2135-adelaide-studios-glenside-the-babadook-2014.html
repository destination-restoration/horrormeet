Filming location · The Babadook (2014)

Adelaide Studios, Glenside

Amelia's Victorian terrace, including the basement and the hallway that swallows the light, was built as a set on the sound stage here. The Babadook was the first feature to shoot in the South Australian Film Corporation's new studios.
