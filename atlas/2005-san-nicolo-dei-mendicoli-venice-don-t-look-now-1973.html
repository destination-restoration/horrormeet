Filming location · Don't Look Now (1973)

San Nicolo dei Mendicoli, Venice

This twelfth century church in a quiet corner of Dorsoduro is the one John Baxter has come to Venice to restore, and where he falls from the scaffolding. It is still a parish church and is usually open to visitors.
