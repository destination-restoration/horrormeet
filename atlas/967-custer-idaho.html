Custer, Idaho

A ghost town in Idaho. Custer is a ghost town in Custer County, Idaho, United States. Established in 1877, it is at 44°23′15″N 114°41′45″W (44.3874133, -114.6959118), at an elevation of 6,470 feet (1,972 m).
