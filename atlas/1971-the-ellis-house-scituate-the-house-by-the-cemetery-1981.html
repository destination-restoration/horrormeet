Filming location · The House by the Cemetery (1981)

The Ellis House, Scituate

This shingled house on Country Way was the exterior of the Freudstein place, with the tomb filled interiors built at a studio in Rome. It is private property used as an art studio, so view it from the road and do not enter the grounds.
