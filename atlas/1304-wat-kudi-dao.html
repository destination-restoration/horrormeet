Wat Kudi Dao

Listed among reportedly haunted places. Wat Kudi Dao (Thai: วัดกุฎีดาว) is a restored Buddhist temple located in the eastern area of Ayutthaya, Thailand, outside the main city island. It represents a significant example of Late Ayutthaya architecture and is historically linked to the Ban Phlu Luang dynasty.
