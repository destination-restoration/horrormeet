Filming location · Diabolique (1955)

Montfort-l'Amaury, Yvelines

Nicole's house, written as being in Niort, was actually filmed in this hill town west of Paris, opposite a building Clouzot had already used in Le Corbeau. Montfort-l'Amaury is a small tourist town with a ruined castle above it.
