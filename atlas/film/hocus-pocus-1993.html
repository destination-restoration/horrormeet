Filming locations

Where was Hocus Pocus (1993) filmed?

6 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Max and Dani's House

    The seaside house where Max sulks about moving to Salem and Dani demands to go trick or treating. A private residence and a pilgrimage site every October, photograph from the street.

  • Ropes Mansion

    Allison's house, where Max talks his way into the Halloween party. Run by the Peabody Essex Museum and its garden out back is free and open to the public.

  • Old Town Hall, Derby Square

    The town hall where Winifred Sanderson takes the stage and puts a spell on all of Salem's parents. The 1816 building still stands and is open to the public.

  • Salem Common

    The green where Max walks Allison home after school and tries out his terrible flirting. Nine acres of public park, and ground zero for Salem in October.

  • Old Burial Hill

    The hilltop graveyard where Jay and Ice shake Max down for his sneakers, filmed in Marblehead rather than Salem. Colonial headstones from the 1600s with a harbor view.

  • Pioneer Village

    The 1693 prologue, Thackery Binx's cottage and the Sandersons' hanging were shot in America's first living history museum.
