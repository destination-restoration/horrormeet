Filming locations

Where was Christine (1983) filmed?

Christine (1983) was filmed at Arnie Cunningham's House, Rockbridge High School and Leigh Cabot's House. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Arnie Cunningham's House

    The South Pasadena home where Arnie parks a wrecked 1958 Plymouth Fury in the driveway and his parents lose it. Private residence, sidewalk views only.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Rockbridge High School

    Van Nuys High supplied the hallways and lockers where Buddy Repperton's crew torments Arnie. A working public school, admire the 1914 facade from Cedros Avenue.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Leigh Cabot's House

    The Monrovia home where Arnie picks up Leigh for their date, shortly before the car decides she is competition. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
