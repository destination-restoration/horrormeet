Filming locations

Where was The Birds (1963) filmed?

The Birds (1963) was filmed at Potter School, St. Teresa of Avila Church and The Tides Wharf. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Potter School

    The Bodega schoolhouse where Melanie sits smoking while the crows silently pile up on the jungle gym behind her. Private property, best seen from Bodega Lane.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • St. Teresa of Avila Church

    The 1860 white clapboard church standing just in front of the schoolhouse in Hitchcock's shots. Hitchcock went to Mass here during the shoot.

    See it on the map · Source: sonomacounty.com

  • The Tides Wharf

    The dockside restaurant where the townspeople argue about the attacks and the gas station goes up outside. The owner made Hitchcock name the town Bodega Bay before he would allow filming.

    See it on the map · Source: sonomacounty.com
