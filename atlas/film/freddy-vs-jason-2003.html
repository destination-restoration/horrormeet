Where was Freddy vs. Jason (2003) filmed?

Freddy vs. Jason (2003) was filmed at Lori's House, 1428 Elm Street, Springwood High School / Kerrisdale Elementary and Buntzen Lake North Beach. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Lori's House, 1428 Elm Street

    This 1930 Vancouver home stood in for 1428 Elm Street as Lori Campbell's house, where Jason folds Trey up in his bed in the film's first big kill. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map

  • Springwood High School / Kerrisdale Elementary

    A 1908 Vancouver elementary school played the Springwood High where Lori and her friends attend class while Freddy and Jason close in.

    See it on the map

  • Buntzen Lake North Beach

    The BC lake that played Camp Crystal Lake for the cornfield-and-lakeside showdown. The cabins were built for the shoot and torn out afterward, but the beach is public.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
