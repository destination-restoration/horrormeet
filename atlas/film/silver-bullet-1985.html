Filming locations

Where was Silver Bullet (1985) filmed?

Silver Bullet (1985) was filmed at Pender County Courthouse square, Burgaw, Ideal Barber Shop, 105 South Wright Street, Burgaw and Greenfield Lake, Wilmington. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Pender County Courthouse square, Burgaw

    The Tarker's Mills town festival was staged around the 1936 courthouse in the middle of Burgaw. The courthouse and square are still the center of the town.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Ideal Barber Shop, 105 South Wright Street, Burgaw

    One of the Tarker's Mills storefronts a block from the courthouse. The barber shop building is still standing in Burgaw's small downtown.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Greenfield Lake, Wilmington

    The fireworks scene where Marty meets the werewolf on a bridge was shot at this cypress lake in Wilmington, and the search party and gazebo scenes were filmed in the surrounding park. The bridge used in the film has since been torn down, but the lake and its loop trail are open to the public.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
