Where was Phantasm (1979) filmed?

Phantasm (1979) was filmed at Dunsmuir House, Oakland, Mike and Jody's house, 1232 Corsica Drive, Fortune teller's house, 22840 Calvert Street, Woodland Hills and 1824 West Main Street, Alhambra. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Dunsmuir House, Oakland

    The white-columned mansion on this estate is Morningside Mortuary, the Tall Man's funeral home, though the interiors were sets built elsewhere. The house and gardens are a City of Oakland historic site open for tours and events.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Mike and Jody's house, 1232 Corsica Drive

    The brothers' home in Don Coscarelli's film sits on a quiet Pacific Palisades street. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE: view from the sidewalk and do not knock.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • Fortune teller's house, 22840 Calvert Street, Woodland Hills

    The house where the old woman and the girl read Mike's fortune and the black box test happens. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE: look from the street only.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • 1824 West Main Street, Alhambra

    The interior of Reggie's ice cream shop was filmed in this West Main Street storefront in Alhambra. The building remains in commercial use.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com
