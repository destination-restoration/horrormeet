Filming locations

Where was Black Sunday (1960) filmed?

2 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Castello Massimo, Arsoli

    Prince Massimo's castle in the hill town of Arsoli east of Rome provided the exterior of the Vajda castle where Princess Asa returns from the grave. The castle is still owned by the Massimo family and opens for limited guided visits.

  • Orto Botanico di Roma

    The garden fountain scene between Princess Katia and her father was filmed in Rome's botanical garden on the slope of the Janiculum. The garden belongs to Sapienza University and is open to the public for an entrance fee.
