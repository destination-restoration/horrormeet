Where was Terrifier 2 (2022) filmed?

Terrifier 2 (2022) was filmed at Abracadabra NYC, QXT's Night Club and Fright Factory. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Abracadabra NYC

    The costume shop where Sienna and Allie run into Art the Clown and he grabs those sunflower sunglasses. A real 40-year-old magic and costume store off the Flatiron.

    See it on the map · Source: fangoria.com

  • QXT's Night Club

    The club scenes were shot in this real Newark goth and industrial venue. Damien Leone picked it for production value he could never have afforded to build.

    See it on the map · Source: thecinemaholic.com

  • Fright Factory

    Art's haunted-house carnage was filmed inside a genuine haunt, 25,000 square feet buried in the basement of a century-old South Philly factory. It opens every Halloween season.

    See it on the map · Source: thecinemaholic.com
