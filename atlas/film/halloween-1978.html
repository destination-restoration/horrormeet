Filming locations

Where was Halloween (1978) filmed?

7 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Original Myers House Lot

    The patch of ground where the Myers house actually sat when Carpenter shot the 1978 opening. The house was hauled away in 1987 and an apartment complex now fills the lot. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

  • Nichols Hardware Store

    The storefront Michael robs for rope, a knife and a Halloween mask before he starts hunting Laurie. It sits directly across Mission Street from where the Myers house was relocated.

  • The Wallace House

    The house across the street where Annie, Bob and Lynda all die, including Bob pinned to the kitchen wall in the original's most famous kill. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

  • Laurie Strode's House

    Laurie's home in the 1978 original, with the corner porch where she sits holding a pumpkin watching the trick-or-treaters. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

  • South Pasadena High School

    Haddonfield High in the 1978 original. The campus Laurie walks out of while Michael's stolen station wagon idles at the curb.

  • Sierra Madre Pioneer Cemetery

    Haddonfield Cemetery, where Loomis and the groundskeeper find Judith Myers' headstone has been taken. The 1881 graveyard is still tucked behind the hedges.

  • Lynda's House and the Hedge

    The Montrose Avenue block where Laurie spots Michael standing beside the hedge, looks away, and finds him gone. Locals have kept that hedge trimmed the same way for decades. Private residence.
