Filming locations

Where was Cat's Eye (1985) filmed?

Cat's Eye (1985) was filmed at Dick Morrison's house, 314 South Front Street, Wilmington, 125 South 3rd Street, Wilmington and Isabel Holmes Bridge, Wilmington. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Dick Morrison's house, 314 South Front Street, Wilmington

    The home of James Woods's character in the Quitters, Inc. segment. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE in Wilmington's historic district: look from the sidewalk and do not knock.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • 125 South 3rd Street, Wilmington

    Saint Stephen's School for the Exceptional, where Morrison's daughter is a student, was shot at this downtown Wilmington building. It still stands in the historic district.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Isabel Holmes Bridge, Wilmington

    The lift bridge over the Northeast Cape Fear River appears in the Quitters, Inc. segment. It is still carrying US 74 traffic into downtown Wilmington.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
