Filming locations

Where was MaXXXine (2024) filmed?

8 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Elks Lodge 2190, Canoga Park

    The Landing Strip club, where Maxine dances early in the film, was shot in this fraternal lodge on Osborne Street. It is a private members club.

  • Maxine's apartment, 6410 Hollywood Boulevard

    Maxine's apartment building and the alley beside it, on the block of Hollywood Boulevard that was closed for four days of shooting. The ground floor is storefronts.

  • 6315 Hollywood Boulevard

    The strip club exterior and interior on the south side of Hollywood Boulevard, dressed back to grimy 1985. The block is now mostly tourist retail.

  • Hollywood Forever Cemetery

    Bodies are pulled from the pond in this cemetery on Santa Monica Boulevard, backing onto Paramount. It is open daily and hosts film screenings in summer.

  • Westin Bonaventure Hotel

    Maxine first meets the private investigator Labat in the revolving lounge at the top of this mirrored downtown hotel. The lobby and lounge are open to visitors.

  • Warner Bros Studios, Burbank

    The film opens on the lot here, with scenes shot outside Stage 15 and Stage 16 and at Gate 5. The studio runs paid tours.

  • Universal Studios backlot

    The chase and climax use the backlot's Western Street, Courthouse Square and the Psycho house and Bates Motel. The backlot is visible from the studio tram tour.

  • TCL Chinese Theatre

    Grauman's Chinese Theatre closes out Maxine's Hollywood story. The forecourt with the handprints is free to walk through.
