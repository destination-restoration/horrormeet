Filming locations

Where was The Walking Dead filmed?

The Walking Dead was filmed at The Woodbury Shoppe, Senoia, Nic and Norman's and Jackson Street Bridge. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • The Woodbury Shoppe, Senoia

    Downtown Senoia is the Governor's Woodbury from Season 3, and this shop on Main Street is the official store sitting inside the set. Cross the tracks and you are in Alexandria.

    See it on the map · Source: exploregeorgia.org

  • Nic and Norman's

    Norman Reedus and Greg Nicotero's own burger joint, opened on the Woodbury set street in Senoia and hung with Reedus's photography.

    See it on the map · Source: exploregeorgia.org

  • Jackson Street Bridge

    The Atlanta skyline view behind Rick Grimes as he rides toward the dead city in the Season 1 pilot. Public bridge, free, and the single most photographed shot in the show.

    See it on the map · Source: fangirlquest.com

Where was The Walking Dead filmed?
The Walking Dead was filmed at The Woodbury Shoppe, Senoia, Nic and Norman's and Jackson Street Bridge. Every location on this page is a real place with its spot on the HorrorMeet Atlas map.

Can you visit the The Walking Dead filming locations?
Many of them, yes. Each location above says whether it is a public place, a private home or no longer standing. Private homes are for looking at from the sidewalk only: never knock, never step onto the property.
