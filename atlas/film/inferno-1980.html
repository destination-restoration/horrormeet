Where was Inferno (1980) filmed?

Inferno (1980) was filmed at Gapstow Bridge, Central Park, Biblioteca Angelica, Rome and Piazza Mincio, Quartiere Coppede, Rome. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Gapstow Bridge, Central Park

    Kazanian drowns a sack of cats in the pond beside Gapstow Bridge, then falls in among the rats himself. The stone bridge at the north end of the Pond is one of Central Park's most photographed spots and is open to walk across.

    See it on the map · Source: imdb.com

  • Biblioteca Angelica, Rome

    The library interiors, where Mark's friend Sara finds the book of The Three Mothers and is followed into the cellars, were shot in this seventeenth century library near Piazza Navona. The Angelica is a working public library and its reading room can be visited.

    See it on the map · Source: imdb.com

  • Piazza Mincio, Quartiere Coppede, Rome

    The exterior entrance used for the library sits in the Coppede quarter, a small pocket of 1920s fantasy architecture around the Fountain of the Frogs. The square is a public street and free to wander.

    See it on the map · Source: imdb.com
