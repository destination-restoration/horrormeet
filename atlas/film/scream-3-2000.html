Where was Scream 3 (2000) filmed?

Scream 3 (2000) was filmed at Harper House, CBS Studio Center and Canfield-Moreno Estate. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Harper House

    Cotton Weary's West Hollywood apartment building, where Scream 3 opens with Ghostface killing him and his girlfriend. The 1929 Harper House is a private residence.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • CBS Studio Center

    Stood in for Sunrise Studios, where Stab 3 is shooting and Ghostface stalks the fake Woodsboro backlot sets. A working studio lot, exterior viewing only.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Canfield-Moreno Estate

    The Paramour Estate in Silver Lake is producer John Milton's mansion, home of the climactic reveal and the final Ghostface showdown. Private event estate.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
