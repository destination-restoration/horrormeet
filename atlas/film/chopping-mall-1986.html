Where was Chopping Mall (1986) filmed?

Chopping Mall (1986) was filmed at Sherman Oaks Galleria and Beverly Center, Los Angeles. 2 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Sherman Oaks Galleria

    The killbot rampage was shot after hours in the enclosed Sherman Oaks Galleria, the mall that also defined Valley Girl and Fast Times at Ridgemont High. The indoor mall closed in 1999 after earthquake damage and was rebuilt as an open-air retail and office center, so the interiors from the film no longer exist.

    See it on the map · Source: en.wikipedia.org

  • Beverly Center, Los Angeles

    The Park Plaza Mall exteriors were shot at the Beverly Center, which had originally been the planned location for the whole film. The mall is still open and was given a major redesign in 2018.

    See it on the map · Source: fast-rewind.com
