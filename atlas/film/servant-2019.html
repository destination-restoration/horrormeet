Filming locations

Where was Servant (2019) filmed?

Servant (2019) was filmed at 2108 Spruce Street, Fitler Square, Philadelphia and Boardwalk at 14th Street, Ocean City, New Jersey. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • 2108 Spruce Street

    The Turner brownstone the whole series revolves around. Interiors were built in a former paint factory in Delaware County, but this is the real front door: a PRIVATE RESIDENCE on a busy Center City block, so view it from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map · Source: phillychitchat.com

  • Fitler Square, Philadelphia

    The small park a few blocks from the Turner house, used for filming in the third season. It is a public square.

    See it on the map · Source: phillychitchat.com

  • Boardwalk at 14th Street, Ocean City, New Jersey

    Apple TV crews filmed beach and boardwalk scenes for a season 3 episode near 14th Street in March 2021, according to the city's spokesman. About 65 cast and crew were based at the nearby Flanders Hotel for the shoot, though the filming itself was on the sand and the boards. The boardwalk is a public place and free to walk.

    See it on the map · Source: patch.com
