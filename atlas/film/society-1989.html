Filming locations

Where was Society (1989) filmed?

Society (1989) was filmed at Tournament House, 391 South Orange Grove Boulevard, Pasadena and Birmingham High School, Van Nuys. 2 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Tournament House, 391 South Orange Grove Boulevard, Pasadena

    The Wrigley Mansion on Pasadena's old Millionaires Row is the house where the shunting party takes place. It is now the Tournament of Roses headquarters and offers free public tours in season.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Birmingham High School, Van Nuys

    Bill Whitney's school scenes were shot at this San Fernando Valley campus. It now operates as Birmingham Community Charter High School.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
