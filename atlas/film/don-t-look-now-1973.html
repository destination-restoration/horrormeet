Filming locations

Where was Don't Look Now (1973) filmed?

Don't Look Now (1973) was filmed at San Nicolo dei Mendicoli, Venice, Hotel Gabrielli, Venice, Bauer Palazzo, Venice and Palazzo Grimani di Santa Maria Formosa, Venice. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • San Nicolo dei Mendicoli, Venice

    This twelfth century church in a quiet corner of Dorsoduro is the one John Baxter has come to Venice to restore, and where he falls from the scaffolding. It is still a parish church and is usually open to visitors.

    See it on the map · Source: bfi.org.uk

  • Hotel Gabrielli, Venice

    The exterior and lobby of the Baxters' Hotel Europa were filmed at the Gabrielli on the Riva degli Schiavoni. The hotel is still operating on the waterfront.

    See it on the map · Source: bfi.org.uk

  • Bauer Palazzo, Venice

    The interiors of the fictional Hotel Europa, including the much discussed love scene, were shot at the Bauer Grunwald near Piazza San Marco. The building still stands as the Bauer Palazzo, which has been closed for a long redevelopment.

    See it on the map · Source: bfi.org.uk

  • Palazzo Grimani di Santa Maria Formosa, Venice

    The palazzo where Baxter finally corners the small figure in the red coat stands beside Santa Maria Formosa. It is now a state museum open to ticketed visitors.

    See it on the map · Source: bfi.org.uk
