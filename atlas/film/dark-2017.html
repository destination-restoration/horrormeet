Filming locations

Where was Dark (2017) filmed?

Dark (2017) was filmed at Marlene Dietrich Halle, Studio Babelsberg, Am Glockenturm, Berlin, Wildenbruch forest, Brandenburg, Fahrtechnik Akademie, Kallinchen and 8 more. 12 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Marlene Dietrich Halle, Studio Babelsberg

    The interior of the Winden nuclear plant, including the reactor hall and the sealed chamber, was built as a set in this hall. Most of the series studio work was done at Babelsberg.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Am Glockenturm, Berlin

    The main gate of the Winden nuclear power plant is this square beside the Olympic Stadium, with cooling towers added digitally behind it. It is a public street.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Wildenbruch forest, Brandenburg

    The entrance to the Winden caves, where the children vanish and the time passage begins, was a fake rock face built in the forest near Wildenbruch. The woods are real, the cave never existed.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Fahrtechnik Akademie, Kallinchen

    The forest road and the bus stop with the lonely traffic light are on the grounds of a driving academy near Zossen. It is private property.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Reinfelder Schule, Berlin

    The Gesamtschule Winden, where Jonas, Martha and Bartosz are pupils. It is a working Berlin school.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Akademie der Kuenste, Berlin

    The modernist arts academy building on the Hanseatenweg plays the Winden police station. It hosts public exhibitions.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Suedwestkirchhof Stahnsdorf

    This huge forest cemetery south of Berlin supplied the chapel used by the Sic Mundus secret society. It is open to visitors.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Einhornhoehle, Harz

    Real cavern interiors for the Winden cave passages were shot in this show cave in the Harz mountains. It runs guided tours.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Schloss Lanke, Brandenburg

    The exterior of the Waldhotel Winden that Regina Tiedemann struggles to keep open. It is a private estate north of Berlin.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Funkhaus Berlin

    The Sic Mundus time machine room was built in a studio of the former East German radio complex on the Spree. The Funkhaus is now a music and events venue.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Ruedersdorf industrial site

    The vast works east of Berlin were used for the derelict factory scenes. Parts of the site are open as a museum park.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • Schloss Boernicke, Bernau

    The Tannhaus clock factory in 1888 is this manor house north east of Berlin.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

Quick answers

Where was Dark (2017) filmed?
Dark (2017) was filmed at Marlene Dietrich Halle, Studio Babelsberg, Am Glockenturm, Berlin, Wildenbruch forest, Brandenburg, Fahrtechnik Akademie, Kallinchen and 8 more. Every location on this page is a real place with its spot on the HorrorMeet Atlas map.

Can you visit the Dark (2017) filming locations?
Many of them, yes. Each location above says whether it is a public place, a private home or no longer standing. Private homes are for looking at from the sidewalk only: never knock, never step onto the property.
