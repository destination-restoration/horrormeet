Where was Salem's Lot (1979) filmed?

Salem's Lot (1979) was filmed at The Marsten House, Barlow and Straker Antiques and Ben Mears' Boarding House. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • The Marsten House

    An ordinary Ferndale home got a nightmare facade bolted onto it to become the Marsten House looming over Tobe Hooper's miniseries. The fake front came off after filming.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Barlow and Straker Antiques

    The Ferndale storefront that became the antique shop run by James Mason's Straker, the vampire's very polite human front man.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Ben Mears' Boarding House

    Eva Miller's boarding house, where writer Ben Mears rents a room while the town quietly turns. Private residence on Ferndale's Ocean Avenue.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
