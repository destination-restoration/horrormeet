Filming locations

Where was Hannibal (2013) filmed?

Hannibal (2013) was filmed at 71 Simcoe Street, University of Toronto Scarborough, David Dunlap Observatory, 50 Lowther Avenue and 6 more. 10 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • 71 Simcoe Street

    Hannibal Lecter's cavernous psychiatric office is the 1873 St Andrew's Church manse in downtown Toronto. It appears throughout all three seasons.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • University of Toronto Scarborough

    The brutalist concrete Humanities Wing on Military Trail plays the FBI Academy at Quantico, including the lecture halls and the Behavioral Analysis Unit. It was designed by John Andrews, architect of the CN Tower.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • David Dunlap Observatory

    The domed 1935 observatory in Richmond Hill stands in, with heavy digital help, for the Baltimore hospital for the criminally insane where Will Graham is held. The grounds are a public park.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • 50 Lowther Avenue

    Exterior of Hannibal's own Baltimore townhouse, the one he cooks and entertains in. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE: look from the sidewalk and never knock.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • 2350 Doulton Drive

    Dr Bedelia Du Maurier's coolly elegant house, on a ravine street in Mississauga. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE: view it from the road only.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • 514 Jarvis Street

    The Rundle House, a Victorian mansion on Jarvis Street, is the home of Tobias the string maker in the episode Fromage. It is a commercial building today.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • Royal Ontario Museum

    The ROM doubles for the Baltimore natural history museum in the season two episode Naka-choko. It is open to the public.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • Casa Loma Stables

    The Edwardian stables behind Casa Loma serve as Mason Verger's pig barns. They are part of the Casa Loma visitor site.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • Rockwood Conservation Area

    The limestone cliffs and river here were used for a body discovery in the second season. It is a public conservation area near Guelph.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • Novotel Toronto Centre

    Dressed with digital scenery to become the European hotel where Hannibal and Bedelia hide in season three. It still operates as a hotel.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com
