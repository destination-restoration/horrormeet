Filming locations

Where was Friday the 13th (1980) filmed?

7 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Blairstown Diner

    The diner where Steve Christy kills time over coffee and pie while his counselors are being butchered back at camp. Still open, still serving, and still throwing a party every Friday the 13th.

  • Roy's Hall / Blairstown Theatre

    Annie strolls past this 1913 movie house on her doomed walk through town in the opening act. Painted red in 1979, bright blue today, facade unmistakable.

  • Blair Creek Bridge

    The bridge Annie crosses heading west out of town, asking anyone who'll listen how to get to Camp Crystal Lake. The stone arches look almost untouched since 1979.

  • Hope General Store

    The old general store where Annie asks for directions to Camp Crystal Lake and gets the look everybody gives her. It has traded as an antiques shop in recent years.

  • Moravian Cemetery

    The black iron cemetery arch where Enos drops Annie off to hitch the rest of the way. The burial ground dates to the 1760s and the gate is still there.

  • Crazy Ralph's Corner

    The corner where Crazy Ralph rolls up on his bicycle to tell Annie she's going to a death curse. Fan pilgrimage stop number one in Hope.

  • Millbrook Road Camp Run

    The rural stretch where Jack, Marcie and Ned barrel toward Camp Crystal Lake in the pickup. Private property lines both sides, shoot it from the road.
