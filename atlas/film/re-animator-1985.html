Filming locations

Where was Re-Animator (1985) filmed?

5 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • West Los Angeles VA Medical Center

    The Wilshire Boulevard veterans hospital provided the exterior of Miskatonic University Medical School. It is still an active VA medical campus.

  • Dan Cain's house, 2238 Canyon Drive

    The house Dan Cain rents and where Herbert West sets up his basement lab. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE on a narrow canyon street: view it from the road only.

  • Dean Halsey's house, 405 South Irving Boulevard

    The Windsor Square home of Dean Halsey and his daughter Megan. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE in a historic district: look from the sidewalk and do not approach.

  • Occidental Studios, 201 North Occidental Boulevard

    The morgue and laboratory sets, including the reanimation scenes, were built on stages at this small independent studio near Echo Park. It is one of the oldest continuously operating studio lots in Los Angeles.

  • 1901 Venice Boulevard

    The opening University of Zurich sequence, where West's first experiment goes wrong, was shot at this building on Venice Boulevard, now Loyola High School. The campus is still in use as a school.
