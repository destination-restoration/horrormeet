Where was A Nightmare on Elm Street 3: Dream Warriors (1987) filmed?

A Nightmare on Elm Street 3: Dream Warriors (1987) was filmed at Westin Hills Psychiatric Hospital / UCLA Royce Hall and St. Brendan Catholic Church. 2 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Westin Hills Psychiatric Hospital / UCLA Royce Hall

    The back of UCLA's Royce Hall doubled as Westin Hills, home of the Dream Warriors, and the adjacent Janss Steps returned as the asylum steps in The Dream Child (1989). Open campus, free to wander.

    See it on the map

  • St. Brendan Catholic Church

    This Gothic Revival church is where Dr. Neil Gordon grabs holy water and a crucifix before setting out to bury Freddy's bones in consecrated ground.

    See it on the map
