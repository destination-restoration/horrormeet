Filming locations

Where was It Follows (2014) filmed?

8 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Redford Theatre

    Jay's date with Hugh happens inside this 1928 atmospheric movie palace on Lahser Road, where she first spots a woman nobody else can see. It still runs classic films with organ shows, staffed by volunteers.

  • Jay's house, 38721 Trafalgar Way

    The Height family home, with the above-ground backyard pool and the bedroom where Jay watches the street. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE on a quiet Sterling Heights cul-de-sac.

  • Annie's house, 1492 Yorkshire Road

    The house in the cold open, where the first girl runs out into the street in heels before driving to the beach. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE in Birmingham, Michigan.

  • Greg's house, 5222 Allison Drive

    Greg's home across the subdivision, seen from Jay's bedroom window and used for the film's most brutal reveal. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE in Troy, Michigan.

  • Nick's Country Oven, Clawson

    The diner-style restaurant on West 14 Mile Road where the friends regroup over food. It is still an operating family restaurant.

  • Clark's Ice Cream, Berkley

    The ice cream shop on 12 Mile Road that the characters visit in daylight, a deliberate piece of ordinary suburbia in a film about being followed. It is still a working ice cream stand.

  • Clawson High School pool

    The indoor swimming pool where the climax was shot, with the plan to electrocute the entity in the water. It is a working high school, so the pool is not open to visitors.

  • Water Works Park, Detroit

    The kids walk toward the pool building through Detroit's Water Works Park on East Jefferson, whose high lift pumping station provides the exterior. The park grounds are city water department property and largely closed to the public.
