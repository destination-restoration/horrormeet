Filming locations

Where was Arachnophobia (1990) filmed?

4 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • 755 Main Street, Cambria

    The Victorian gingerbread funeral home where the spider escapes from the coffin stands at the back of this property, facing Arlington Street. Cambria plays the town of Canaima.

  • 4090 Burton Drive, Cambria

    Dr Ross Jennings' new medical practice, virtually unchanged since filming.

  • 1958 Richard Avenue, Cambria

    The Kendalls' house, where a spider gets into the popcorn during Wheel of Fortune. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE: view from the street only.

  • Halter Ranch, Paso Robles

    The house of neighbour Margaret, the spider's first victim, is now part of the Halter Ranch Vineyard on Adelaida Road. The winery has a tasting room.
