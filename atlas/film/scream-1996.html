Filming locations

Where was Scream (1996) filmed?

9 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Casey Becker's House

    The white farmhouse where Drew Barrymore answers the phone, flunks the horror trivia quiz and ends up hanging from a tree. Private residence, admire from the roadside.

  • Stu Macher's House

    The Spring Hill dairy ranch house outside Tomales that hosted the party from hell and the original Ghostface unmasking. Private property that occasionally opens for Scream events, never just turn up.

  • Woodsboro High School

    The Sonoma Community Center played Woodsboro High after Santa Rosa High's board read the script and pulled out. Where Sidney gets suspended and Principal Himbry meets the sharp end.

  • Bradley Video Store

    Randy Meeks' video store, where he lays down the rules of surviving a horror movie. The video shop is long gone and the building now houses a restaurant.

  • Tatum Riley's House

    The McDonald Avenue home where Tatum lives with big brother Dewey, and where Sidney hides out after the first attack. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

  • Sidney Prescott's House

    Sidney's home on Calistoga Road, site of the closet attack and the hello-Sidney phone call that starts it all. Behind a locked gate on private land.

  • Woodsboro Town Square

    Healdsburg's plaza doubled for downtown Woodsboro, where the news vans camp out and the town reels after the murders. Fully public and barely changed since 1996.

  • Woodsboro Police Station

    The Healdsburg police station stood in for the Woodsboro PD where Billy is hauled in after Casey's murder. Working police building, be respectful outside.

  • Pacific Market

    The grocery store where Sidney and Tatum shop for the party while Woodsboro is under curfew. Still a working neighborhood market.
