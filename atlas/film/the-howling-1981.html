Where was The Howling (1981) filmed?

The Howling (1981) was filmed at Mendocino Woodlands State Park, Russian Gulch State Park, Mendocino County, Paradise Cove, Malibu, 6607 Hollywood Boulevard and 2 more. 6 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Mendocino Woodlands State Park

    Joe Dante took over Camp 2, the most remote of the three 1930s work camps here, to shoot the Colony where the werewolves live. The camps are still standing and can be rented as group retreats.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Russian Gulch State Park, Mendocino County

    The redwood forest running and chase footage was shot in the coastal woods just north of Mendocino village. It is still a state park open for day use and camping.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Paradise Cove, Malibu

    The beach campfire sequence when Karen first arrives at the Colony was shot at this Malibu cove. It is a private beach club and parking lot but remains accessible for a fee.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • 6607 Hollywood Boulevard

    The adult bookstore where Karen goes looking for Eddie Quist was this Hollywood Boulevard storefront. The block has been heavily redeveloped and the shop is long gone.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Vermont Canyon Tennis Courts, Griffith Park, Los Angeles

    Karen and Donna play tennis here, and Sheriff Sam Newfield reassures them about the creature they heard in the woods. The scene is set at the Colony but was shot on a court at 2715 North Vermont Canyon Road, in the south of Griffith Park. The courts are a public city tennis facility, so visitors can go.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Site of Eddie Quist's house, Garland Avenue, Los Angeles

    Chris and Terry search Eddie Quist's room, its walls covered in drawings, in an old Queen Anne house that stood on this block of Garland Avenue between 7th and 8th Streets, behind 1200 West 7th Street. The house was demolished right after filming in 1980 and nothing of it remains. The pin marks the block, not an exact lot; it is a public city street.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
