Where was 28 Days Later (2002) filmed?

28 Days Later (2002) was filmed at Westminster Bridge, Horse Guards Parade, Shaftesbury Memorial Fountain, Piccadilly Circus, Centre Point, New Oxford Street and 8 more. 12 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Westminster Bridge

    Jim crosses the empty bridge toward the Houses of Parliament in the film's most copied image. The crew held traffic for a few minutes at a time in the early hours on Sundays to get it.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Horse Guards Parade

    Part of Jim's walk through a deserted central London after he wakes in the hospital. The parade ground is open to the public and still used for ceremonial duties.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Shaftesbury Memorial Fountain, Piccadilly Circus

    The fountain, commonly called Eros, appears boarded up and covered in missing person notices. It sits in the middle of Piccadilly Circus and is one of the easiest shots in the film to stand in.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Centre Point, New Oxford Street

    The 1960s tower at St Giles Circus features in the abandoned West End sequence. It was converted into apartments in the 2010s.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Royal Exchange, City of London

    Jim wanders through the deserted financial district past the Royal Exchange on Cornhill. The building now houses shops and restaurants under its portico.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • St Anne's Church, Limehouse

    The Hawksmoor church where Jim finds bodies in the pews and is attacked by the infected priest. It is a working parish church in Tower Hamlets.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Limehouse Link tunnel

    The tunnel chase, with the taxi reversing away from the infected in the dark, was shot here rather than the Blackwall Tunnel. It is a busy road tunnel, so this one is for looking at from street level.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • 145 Tottenham Court Road

    The supermarket looting scene, where the survivors calmly stock up on single malt, was shot in what was then a Budgens. The shop has since become a Lidl.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Central Middlesex Hospital, Acton Lane

    Part of the deserted hospital where Jim wakes from his coma, cut together with the now demolished Greenwich District Hospital. Central Middlesex is still an NHS hospital site.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Waverley Abbey, Surrey

    The ruined Cistercian abbey near Farnham where the group rests on the road north. The ruins are free to visit and looked after by English Heritage.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Trafalgar House, Wiltshire

    The country mansion that Major West's soldiers hold as their blockade headquarters, five miles south of Salisbury. It is a PRIVATE HOUSE, hired out for weddings and events rather than open for tours.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Ennerdale Water, Cumbria

    The final act, with the cottage and the enormous cloth sign laid out for the passing jet, was shot around this Lake District lake. It is the quietest of the major lakes and has no road along its shore.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

Quick answers

Where was 28 Days Later (2002) filmed?
28 Days Later (2002) was filmed at Westminster Bridge, Horse Guards Parade, Shaftesbury Memorial Fountain, Piccadilly Circus, Centre Point, New Oxford Street and 8 more. Every location on this page is a real place with its spot on the HorrorMeet Atlas map.

Can you visit the 28 Days Later (2002) filming locations?
Many of them, yes. Each location above says whether it is a public place, a private home or no longer standing. Private homes are for looking at from the sidewalk only: never knock, never step onto the property.
