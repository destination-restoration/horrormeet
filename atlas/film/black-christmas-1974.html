Where was Black Christmas (1974) filmed?

Black Christmas (1974) was filmed at 6 Clarendon Crescent, Toronto, 97 Main Street, Toronto, Seeley Hall, Trinity College, Toronto and Soldiers' Tower, University of Toronto. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • 6 Clarendon Crescent, Toronto

    The Pi Kappa Sigma sorority house, with Billy hiding in the attic. This is a PRIVATE HOME on a tiny private road: look from the sidewalk and never knock.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • 97 Main Street, Toronto

    The Bedford police precinct, with the Main and Swanwick street sign that is genuinely there. The building is Community Centre 55 in East Danforth.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Seeley Hall, Trinity College, Toronto

    Where Peter plays and then smashes up the piano. Trinity College is part of the University of Toronto.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Soldiers' Tower, University of Toronto

    Mr Harrison waits in vain for his daughter beside Hart House, with the Trinity College tower visible through the arch. The 1924 tower is a First World War memorial.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
