Where was Escape from New York (1981) filmed?

Escape from New York (1981) was filmed at Sepulveda Dam, Los Angeles, ArtCenter College of Design Hillside Campus, Pasadena, The Wiltern, 3790 Wilshire Boulevard, Fabulous Fox Theatre, St. Louis and 5 more. 9 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Sepulveda Dam, Los Angeles

    The 1941 flood-control dam is the brutalist concrete structure standing in for the air traffic control center and the fortified bridge out of Manhattan. It is still in place in the Sepulveda Basin and visible from the recreation area.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • ArtCenter College of Design Hillside Campus, Pasadena

    The long steel-and-glass building on Lida Street gave the film its cold institutional corridors during Snake Plissken's briefing. It is still an active art and design college campus.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • The Wiltern, 3790 Wilshire Boulevard

    The 1931 Wiltern Theatre and its basement supplied interiors for the World Trade Center sequences. It was saved from demolition in the 1980s and now operates as a concert venue.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Fabulous Fox Theatre, St. Louis

    The Fox facade appears as a derelict Broadway theatre, and it was genuinely rundown when the crew shot it. It has since been fully restored and is one of the busiest theatres in the Midwest.

    See it on the map · Source: en.wikipedia.org

  • New Masonic Temple, 3681 Lindell Boulevard, St. Louis

    The columned Masonic temple stood in for the New York Public Library that Plissken searches for the President. The Masons sold the building in 2014 and it has been converted to apartments.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • St. Louis Union Station

    The disused train sheds and the Grand Hall provided the railway scenes and a major fight. The station has since been converted into a hotel, aquarium and retail complex.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Old Chain of Rocks Bridge, St. Louis

    The mined 69th Street Bridge that Plissken drives across in the finale is this kinked 1929 span over the Mississippi. It was closed to cars in 1968 and reopened in 1999 as a pedestrian and bicycle bridge on Route 66.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Doheny Memorial Library, USC

    The lobby of USC's 1932 main library doubled for the interior of the New York Public Library. It is still the university's central library.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Century Plaza Towers, Century City

    The twin triangular towers stand in for the entrance to the World Trade Center in the film's opening. Both towers still dominate the Century City skyline.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
