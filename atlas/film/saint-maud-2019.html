Filming locations

Where was Saint Maud (2019) filmed?

Saint Maud (2019) was filmed at Vincent Pier lighthouse, Scarborough, Foreshore Road, Scarborough and Holbeck cliffs, Scarborough. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Vincent Pier lighthouse, Scarborough

    The white lighthouse at the end of Vincent Pier in Scarborough's South Bay appears in Maud's wanderings. You can walk out to it around the harbour.

    See it on the map · Source: filmedinyorkshire.co.uk

  • Foreshore Road, Scarborough

    The seafront strip of vintage amusement arcades and chip shops that gives the film its off-season seaside dread. It is busy in summer and eerie in February, which is the version the film uses.

    See it on the map · Source: filmedinyorkshire.co.uk

  • Holbeck cliffs, Scarborough

    The crumbling cliffs at the south end of Scarborough, used for the film's exposed clifftop scenes. This is the slope that gave way in 1993 and took the Holbeck Hall Hotel with it.

    See it on the map · Source: filmedinyorkshire.co.uk
