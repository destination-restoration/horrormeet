Where was A Girl Walks Home Alone at Night (2014) filmed?

A Girl Walks Home Alone at Night (2014) was filmed at Taft, California. 1 real location below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open it on the live map

  • Taft, California

    This small Kern County oil town, about 30 miles southwest of Bakersfield, played the Iranian ghost town of Bad City in Ana Lily Amirpour's black and white vampire western. The nodding pumpjacks and empty streets were shot in and around town.
