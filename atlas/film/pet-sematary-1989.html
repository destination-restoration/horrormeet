Filming locations

Where was Pet Sematary (1989) filmed?

3 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • The Creed House

    The yellow farmhouse with the wraparound porch where Louis Creed moves his family, right off the truck route that kills Church and Gage. Private residence, admire from the road.

  • Jud Crandall's House

    Directly across Point Road from the Creeds, where Fred Gwynne's Jud sits on the porch and tells Louis about the burying ground beyond the deadfall. Private residence.

  • Mount Hope Cemetery

    America's second-oldest garden cemetery, used for Gage's funeral and the night Louis digs his son back up. Stephen King himself shows up here as the minister.
