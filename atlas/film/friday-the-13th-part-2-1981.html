Filming locations

Where was Friday the 13th Part 2 (1981) filmed?

5 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Alice's Apartment Interior

    The building whose interiors became Alice's apartment, where Jason collects her head in the Part 2 prologue. Now a behavioral health center, admire from the sidewalk.

  • Jeff's Truck Tow Spot

    Where Jeff and Sandra's truck gets hooked and hauled off in New Preston, stranding them right where you'd least want to be stranded.

  • Church Street, New Preston

    The village corner where Ted greets Jeff and Sandra as they roll into town. The New Preston streetscape is remarkably unchanged from 1981.

  • Packanack Lodge Counselor Training Center

    The counselor training center where Paul, Ginny and the rest camp out next door to Crystal Lake. The film's buildings were demolished in 2009, so this is a ghost stop.

  • North Spectacle Pond

    Connecticut's stand-in for Crystal Lake, the water Jeff and Sandra hike past on their way to the forbidden camp. The shoreline still reads exactly like the film.
