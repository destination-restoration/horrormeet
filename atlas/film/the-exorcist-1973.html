Where was The Exorcist (1973) filmed?

The Exorcist (1973) was filmed at The MacNeil House, The Tombs, Georgetown University and Martin's Tavern. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • The MacNeil House

    The Georgetown corner house where Regan's bedroom window looked out over the drop; a fake wing was built onto it so the fall would land on the steps. Private residence.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • The Tombs

    The Georgetown pub where Father Karras drinks and wrestles with his faith, and it shows up again in Exorcist III. Still open, still pouring.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Georgetown University

    Healy Hall and Dahlgren Chapel play themselves, and the chapel is where the statue of the Virgin turns up desecrated. The campus doubled as itself again for Exorcist III.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Martin's Tavern

    The Wisconsin Avenue bar where Karras and Father Dyer drink together, a 1933 Georgetown institution that has barely changed.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
