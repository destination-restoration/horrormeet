Filming locations

Where was In the Mouth of Madness (1994) filmed?

In the Mouth of Madness (1994) was filmed at Cathedral of the Transfiguration, Markham, West Montrose Covered Bridge, Ontario, The Unionville Arms, 189 Main Street Unionville, R.C. Harris Water Treatment Plant, Toronto and 1 more. 5 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Cathedral of the Transfiguration, Markham

    The Byzantine-style Slovak cathedral, with its tall bell tower, is the black church of Hobb's End. The building still stands in Markham's Cathedraltown but has been closed and deconsecrated.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • West Montrose Covered Bridge, Ontario

    Ontario's last surviving covered bridge, built in 1881, is the crossing Trent and Linda pass through on the night ride into Hobb's End. It is still open to one lane of traffic over the Grand River.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • The Unionville Arms, 189 Main Street Unionville

    This pub on Unionville's heritage main street is the Hobb's End bar exterior. It is still a working pub and the street's nineteenth-century frontages are largely intact.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • R.C. Harris Water Treatment Plant, Toronto

    The Art Deco waterworks on the Scarborough bluffs plays the exterior of the mental hospital where John Trent is locked up. It is a working treatment plant and the grounds are open as public parkland.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • Eglinton Theatre, 400 Eglinton Avenue West

    The 1936 Art Deco cinema is where Trent sits laughing at the film of his own story in the final scene. It closed as a movie house in 2002 and is now the Eglinton Grand event venue.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com
