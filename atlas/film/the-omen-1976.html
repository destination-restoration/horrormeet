Filming locations

Where was The Omen (1976) filmed?

4 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Pyrford Court

    The Surrey manor that played Pershore Hall, including the birthday party where Damien's nanny hangs herself off the roof shouting it's all for you, Damien. Private estate.

  • All Saints Church, Fulham

    The riverside church where the lightning rod comes loose and impales Father Brennan on the path below. It sits right at the north end of Putney Bridge.

  • Guildford Cathedral

    The modernist brick cathedral on Stag Hill used for the wedding sequence where Damien first loses his mind at the sight of a church. Open to visitors.

  • Brookwood Cemetery

    The American Military Cemetery section of Britain's largest graveyard, standing in for the grim burial ground where Thorn and Jennings dig up a jackal. Open to the public.
