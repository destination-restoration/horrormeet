Where was 28 Years Later (2025) filmed?

28 Years Later (2025) was filmed at Church of St Mary and St Finnan, Glenfinnan, St John the Baptist, Stanwick St John, Holy Island of Lindisfarne, Lindisfarne causeway and 7 more. 11 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Church of St Mary and St Finnan, Glenfinnan

    The exterior of Jimmy's father's church, above Loch Shiel and a short walk from the Harry Potter viaduct. It is a working Catholic church.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • St John the Baptist, Stanwick St John

    The interior of the church in the prologue, where the children hide as the infected come through the door, was shot in this isolated North Yorkshire church. It is usually open to visitors.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Holy Island of Lindisfarne

    The quarantined island community was filmed on Lindisfarne off the Northumberland coast. Check the tide tables before crossing, because the causeway floods twice a day.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Lindisfarne causeway

    The tidal crossing that keeps the survivors safe and traps them in equal measure. The road is submerged at high tide and people are rescued off it every year.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Jubilee Hall, Newton-on-the-Moor

    The village hall five miles south of Alnwick, with its barrel ceiling and wooden beams, played the island community's meeting place. It is still the local community hall.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Kielder Forest

    The woodland training and chase scenes were shot in England's largest planted forest, with its moss covered hills. There are waymarked trails and a visitor centre.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Kielder Water

    The reservoir and its dam works appear in the sequence where the Swedish soldiers are attacked. It is the largest artificial lake in northern Europe and ringed by a public path.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Sycamore Gap, Hadrian's Wall

    The dip in Hadrian's Wall beside Crag Lough, walked in the film's journey south. The famous tree that stood here was ILLEGALLY FELLED in September 2023, so only the stump and the wall remain.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Fountains Abbey, Ripon

    The travellers shelter overnight in these Cistercian ruins, founded in 1132. The abbey is a World Heritage Site run by the National Trust and is ticketed.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Embsay station, Embsay and Bolton Abbey Steam Railway

    The abandoned train is a heritage railway east of Skipton, which runs four miles of preserved line. It operates on public running days.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Cheddar Gorge, Somerset

    The limestone gorge in the Mendip Hills stands in for a solo stretch of the journey. The B3135 winds through it and the cliffs are open to walk.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
