Where was Obsession (2026) filmed?

Obsession (2026) was filmed at Cadillac Jack's at the Pink Motel, Cassell's Music, The Roguelike Tavern, Little Toni's and 1 more. 5 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Cadillac Jack's at the Pink Motel

    The opening coffee-shop scene where Bear runs lines with his friends was shot inside Cadillac Jack's, the 1949 diner attached to the famous Pink Motel. It is a standing film set, not a working restaurant, so peek through the windows.

    See it on the map

  • Cassell's Music

    The music store where Bear, Nikki, Sarah and Ian work is the real Cassell's Music, the same shop from Wayne's World. It closed in 2025 after nearly 80 years, about a year after the shoot wrapped.

    See it on the map

  • The Roguelike Tavern

    The trivia-night bar scene was filmed at this immersive-entertainment tavern on Ventura Boulevard. The venue has since closed at this address while it relocates.

    See it on the map

  • Little Toni's

    Bear and Nikki's dinner date, where her behavior first turns strange, was shot at this old-school Italian pizza joint that has been slinging pies since 1956. Still open, still red-sauce perfect.

    See it on the map

  • The Green Man

    The crystal shop where Bear buys the One Wish Willow is a real metaphysical supply store and apothecary in Burbank, stocked with incense, herbs, candles and crystals. Open to the public.

    See it on the map
