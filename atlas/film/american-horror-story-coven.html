Where was American Horror Story: Coven filmed?

American Horror Story: Coven was filmed at Buckner Mansion / Miss Robichaux's Academy, LaLaurie Mansion, Gallier House / LaLaurie Mansion Exterior, Hermann-Grima House / LaLaurie Mansion Interior and 1 more. 5 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Buckner Mansion / Miss Robichaux's Academy

    This 1856 cotton-baron mansion in the Garden District is Miss Robichaux's Academy for Exceptional Young Ladies, home base for the Coven witches (and it returns in Apocalypse). Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map

  • LaLaurie Mansion

    The real French Quarter mansion of Madame Delphine LaLaurie, the true-crime torturer Kathy Bates plays in Coven. The show did not film inside; it used the Gallier House down the block for exteriors. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map

  • Gallier House / LaLaurie Mansion Exterior

    The 1857 Gallier House museum, a few doors from the real LaLaurie Mansion, stood in for the exterior of Madame LaLaurie's home in Coven. Open for tours, so you can actually walk the gallery.

    See it on the map

  • Hermann-Grima House / LaLaurie Mansion Interior

    The 1831 Hermann-Grima House provided the interiors of Madame LaLaurie's mansion in Coven, including the attic torture-chamber scenes. A museum, so you can step inside the horror.

    See it on the map

  • Lafayette Cemetery No. 1

    The Garden District's famous above-ground cemetery hosted Coven's jazz funeral and the Test of the Seven Wonders scenes. Historic city cemetery; check current tour access.

    See it on the map
