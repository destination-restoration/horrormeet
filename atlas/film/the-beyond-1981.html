Filming locations

Where was The Beyond (1981) filmed?

4 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Fairview-Riverside State Park, Madisonville

    The Victorian Otis House inside this Louisiana state park was the Seven Doors Hotel, the building sitting on one of the gateways to hell. The park is open to visitors and the house can be toured, with the hotel interiors having been built on a Rome sound stage.

  • 1221 Orange Street, New Orleans

    The house where the blind girl Emily lives with her dog Dickie is a real home in the Lower Garden District. This is a private residence, so look at it from the sidewalk and do not knock.

  • Dillard University, New Orleans

    The white colonnaded buildings of this historically Black university stood in for the hospital where Dr McCabe works. Dillard is an active campus, so visitors should stay on the public walkways.

  • Cornstalk Hotel, New Orleans

    The hotel and its famous cast iron cornstalk fence appear behind Liza and McCabe as they walk through the French Quarter to the bookshop. It is still a working hotel on Royal Street and the fence is visible from the pavement.
