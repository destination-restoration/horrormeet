Where was Night of the Creeps (1986) filmed?

Night of the Creeps (1986) was filmed at 716 West 28th Street sorority house, Haines Hall, UCLA and California State University Northridge. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • 716 West 28th Street sorority house

    The main Corman University sorority house is one of the old fraternity row houses just north of USC. These are PRIVATE student houses: view from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • Haines Hall, UCLA

    Campus scenes at Corman University were shot in front of this 1929 Romanesque building on UCLA's original quad. It is still an academic building and the quad is open to visitors.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • California State University Northridge

    Further Corman University campus footage was shot on the CSUN campus in the San Fernando Valley. The university is still in operation, though much of it was rebuilt after the 1994 Northridge earthquake.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com
