Filming locations

Where was Scream 4 (2011) filmed?

6 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Jill Roberts' House

    Sidney's cousin Jill lives here in Ann Arbor's Burns Park, where Sidney comes home to a new generation of Woodsboro victims. Private residence.

  • Olivia Morris' House

    The house next door to Jill's, where Ghostface guts Olivia while Sidney and Jill watch helplessly from the window. Private residence, sidewalk only.

  • Woodsboro Bookstore

    The Next Chapter Bookstore in downtown Northville is where Sidney signs copies of Out of Darkness and Ghostface starts calling again. Public storefront.

  • Woodsboro High School

    Woodworth Middle School in Dearborn served as Woodsboro High, including the Cinema Club scenes and the parking lot attack. Active school, view from the street.

  • Stab-a-thon Barn

    The rural barn where Charlie Walker throws the Stab-a-thon marathon that turns into a bloodbath. Private farmland outside Saline.

  • Kirby Reed's House

    The Northville house that hosts the Stab-a-thon after-party and the brutal final act, including Kirby's horror-trivia standoff at the front door. Private residence.
