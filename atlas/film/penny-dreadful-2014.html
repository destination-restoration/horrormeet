Filming locations

Where was Penny Dreadful (2014) filmed?

6 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Ardmore Studios, Bray

    All three seasons were built and shot here, including a working two storey Victorian mansion for Sir Malcolm Murray's house. Ardmore is a working studio and not open to visitors.

  • Henrietta Street, Dublin

    This Georgian cul-de-sac off Bolton Street played Victorian London, including the exterior of Sir Malcolm's home. Painted period shop signage from the shoot survived on the street for years afterwards.

  • Dame Lane, Dublin

    In February 2014 this narrow city centre lane was dressed as Victorian London's Pulteney Lane. It is an ordinary pub lane today.

  • King's Inns, Dublin

    The 18th century law society building stood in for the Explorers' Club where Sir Malcolm is feted. It is a working legal institution.

  • Powerscourt Estate, Enniskerry

    One of the Wicklow houses used to build the show's Victorian England. The house and its terraced gardens are open to visitors.

  • Kilruddery House, Bray

    The Brabazon family's Elizabethan revival house in County Wicklow supplied period interiors and exteriors. It opens to the public seasonally.
