Filming locations

Where was Drag Me to Hell (2009) filmed?

5 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Pacific Wilshire Bank, 18421 Ventura Boulevard

    Christine refuses Mrs Ganush her loan extension inside what was then an empty bank building in Tarzana. The space has since been in use as a gym.

  • Cal State Northridge

    Clay Dalton lectures in the psychology department, filmed on the CSUN campus in the San Fernando Valley. The campus is open to walk through.

  • Doheny Mansion, Chester Place

    The medium's gothic house, where the seance goes very badly, is the 1899 Doheny Mansion on what is now the Mount St Mary's University Doheny campus. It opens for occasional booked tours rather than drop-in visits.

  • Christine's home, 1031 Everett Street

    The hillside house above Everett Park where the Lamia torments Christine, also used in Training Day. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE.

  • Los Angeles Union Station

    The station where Christine thinks she is leaving her curse behind and meets Clay on the platform. The 1939 concourse is open to the public.
