Filming locations

Where was Goosebumps (1995) filmed?

5 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Casa Loma, Toronto

    The Gothic Revival folly played the medieval fortress in A Night in Terror Tower. It is open daily as a museum.

  • Camp Samac, Oshawa

    The Scout camp north of Oshawa was Camp Nightmoon in Welcome to Camp Nightmare. It is still an active Scouts Canada camp.

  • Distillery District, Toronto

    The Victorian brick lanes of the old Gooderham and Worts distillery appear in Say Cheese and Die. It is now a pedestrian arts and shopping district.

  • 494 Whitevale Road, Pickering

    Carly Beth's house in the very first episode, The Haunted Mask. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE in a tiny heritage hamlet: look from the road only.

  • Mimico Memorial Park, Etobicoke

    Used for Be Careful What You Wish For in the second season. It is a public neighbourhood park.
