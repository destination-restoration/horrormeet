Filming locations

Where was A Nightmare on Elm Street 4: The Dream Master (1988) filmed?

4 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Crave Inn Diner / Cafe Laurent

    Alice and Debbie waitress at the Crave Inn, named for Wes Craven, where time freezes and Freddy serves up a pizza topped with screaming meatball souls. The same diner returns in The Dream Child (1989). Still a working cafe.

  • Alice and Rick Johnson's House

    Home of Dream Master Alice Johnson and her brother Rick, where Alice practices her mirror pep talks and absorbs her fallen friends' powers. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

  • Springwood High School / Venice High

    Venice High School played Springwood High for The Dream Master, the campus where Sheila's asthma attack turns deadly in class. Its famous statue also fronted Rydell High in Grease.

  • Rialto Theatre

    The 1925 movie palace where Alice gets literally pulled through the screen into Freddy's diner dream. Also seen in The Player and Scream 2.
