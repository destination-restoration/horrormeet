Where was A Nightmare on Elm Street (2010) filmed?

A Nightmare on Elm Street (2010) was filmed at Nancy Holbrook's House, 1428 Elm Street, Springwood High School / Elk Grove High, Badham Preschool and Bluff City Cemetery. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Nancy Holbrook's House, 1428 Elm Street

    The remake's 1428 Elm Street, where Rooney Mara's Nancy fights to stay awake and drags Jackie Earle Haley's Freddy into the real world for the finale. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map

  • Springwood High School / Elk Grove High

    Elk Grove High School played the remake's Springwood High, the classroom and hallways where Nancy and Quentin start seeing Freddy in broad daylight.

    See it on the map

  • Badham Preschool

    The abandoned preschool on Chicago's East Side where Nancy and Quentin uncover Freddy's hidden basement room and the truth about what he did to the kids.

    See it on the map

  • Bluff City Cemetery

    Dean's funeral was filmed in this Elgin cemetery, where Kris first spots the photo that proves the friends all knew each other as preschoolers.

    See it on the map
