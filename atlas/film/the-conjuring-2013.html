Where was The Conjuring (2013) filmed?

The Conjuring (2013) was filmed at The Perron Farmhouse, The Warren House and Occult Museum, Father Gordon's Church and UNC Wilmington, DePaolo Hall. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • The Perron Farmhouse

    Every exterior of the Perron family farmhouse, including the bird-slamming and cellar-door scares, was shot at this house on the Black River, not in Rhode Island. Private residence, admire from the road.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • The Warren House and Occult Museum

    Ed and Lorraine's home, the one with the locked room full of cursed objects and the Annabelle case, is this Forest Hills house in Wilmington. Private residence.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Father Gordon's Church

    First Baptist Church stands in for the parish where the Warrens beg for an exorcism approval that never arrives in time. Still an active downtown church.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • UNC Wilmington, DePaolo Hall

    The lecture-hall scenes where the Warrens present their case files to a packed room of students.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

Quick answers

Where was The Conjuring (2013) filmed?
The Conjuring (2013) was filmed at The Perron Farmhouse, The Warren House and Occult Museum, Father Gordon's Church and UNC Wilmington, DePaolo Hall. Every location on this page is a real place with its spot on the HorrorMeet Atlas map.

Can you visit the The Conjuring (2013) filming locations?
Many of them, yes. Each location above says whether it is a public place, a private home or no longer standing. Private homes are for looking at from the sidewalk only: never knock, never step onto the property.
