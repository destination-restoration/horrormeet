Where was 28 Weeks Later (2007) filmed?

28 Weeks Later (2007) was filmed at CityPoint, Ropemaker Street, London, Charing Cross station, London, Wembley Stadium, London, Trafalgar Square, London and 1 more. 5 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • CityPoint, Ropemaker Street, London

    Most of the fortified District One, where returning survivors are housed under army guard, was shot around the CityPoint tower near Moorgate rather than in Canary Wharf itself. The tower is a working office block with a public plaza at its base.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Charing Cross station, London

    The film used the mainline station and the Underground beneath it for the evacuation and the pitch black tunnel sequence. Charing Cross is an active station in daily use.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Wembley Stadium, London

    The empty stadium appears in the sweep over an abandoned London, filmed while the rebuilt Wembley was newly finished. It is open for stadium tours and events.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Trafalgar Square, London

    The production closed central London at dawn to shoot the deserted square, part of the sequence of an emptied capital. The square is public and open at all hours.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Canary Wharf DLR station, London

    The Docklands Light Railway and its arrival at Canary Wharf feature in the scenes of the repopulated safe zone. The station is on the working DLR network.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
