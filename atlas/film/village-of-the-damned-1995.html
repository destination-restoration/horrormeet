Filming locations

Where was Village of the Damned (1995) filmed?

Village of the Damned (1995) was filmed at Point Reyes Station as Midwich, McGowan house, 281 B Street, Point Reyes Station, Nicasio School, 5555 Nicasio Valley Road, Garage at 12786 Sir Francis Drake Boulevard, Inverness and 1 more. 5 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Point Reyes Station as Midwich

    Carpenter came back to the town he used in The Fog and made its main street the town of Midwich, including the blackout sequence. The storefronts are still standing and largely unchanged.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • McGowan house, 281 B Street, Point Reyes Station

    The home of Christopher Reeve's Dr. Alan Chaffee and his family sits a block off the main street. This is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE: look from the sidewalk and do not knock.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Nicasio School, 5555 Nicasio Valley Road

    The town picnic and school fair scenes were shot at this one-room-style country school in Nicasio Valley. It is still an operating public elementary school, so keep to the road.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Garage at 12786 Sir Francis Drake Boulevard, Inverness

    John Carpenter takes his own cameo as a garage attendant at this spot in Inverness. The building is still in commercial use on the main road through town.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Church of the Assumption, Tomales

    The white wooden church and its hillside graveyard in Tomales were used for the cemetery scenes. The church is still standing above the village on Church Street.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
