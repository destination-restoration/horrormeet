Where was House (1986) filmed?

House (1986) was filmed at Mills View, 329 Melrose Avenue, Monrovia. 1 real location below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open it on the live map

  • Mills View, 329 Melrose Avenue, Monrovia

    Roger Cobb's inherited haunted house is this 1887 Victorian in Monrovia, which the crew repainted, fenced and spiked with roof spires for the shoot. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE: view it from Melrose Avenue and do not knock.
