Filming locations

Where was Creepshow (1982) filmed?

Creepshow (1982) was filmed at 361 Willow Hedge Drive, Monroeville, Margaret Morrison Carnegie Hall, Carnegie Mellon University and Island Beach State Park, New Jersey. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • 361 Willow Hedge Drive, Monroeville

    The suburban house where Billy's father throws away the horror comic in the wraparound story. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE in a Monroeville neighborhood whose owners get regular fan traffic: view it from the street and do not knock.

    See it on the map · Source: pittsburghmagazine.com

  • Margaret Morrison Carnegie Hall, Carnegie Mellon University

    The university basement where the crate is found in The Crate was shot in this rotunda-fronted 1907 building in Pittsburgh. It is still a working CMU academic building.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Island Beach State Park, New Jersey

    Something to Tide You Over, in which Leslie Nielsen buries two people up to their necks in the sand, was shot on this barrier-island beach. The park is still open for swimming and surf fishing.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
