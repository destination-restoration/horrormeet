Filming locations

Where was Tremors (1990) filmed?

Tremors (1990) was filmed at Alabama Hills, Lone Pine, Olancha, California, Darwin, California and Perfection town set site, off Cactus Flats Road, Olancha. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Alabama Hills, Lone Pine

    The boulder outcrops where Val and Earl run from the graboids are these weathered granite hills below Mount Whitney, a location used in Westerns since the 1920s. The area is now a designated National Scenic Area with open public access.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Olancha, California

    The town of Perfection was built from scratch on the flats off Cactus Flat Road east of Olancha. The set was torn down after filming, leaving only the dirt road and a replica City of Perfection sign put up by fans.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Darwin, California

    Parts of the film were shot in this near-ghost town in the hills above Panamint Valley, and residents have kept a small museum of Tremors material. Darwin still has a handful of inhabitants and no services.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Perfection town set site, off Cactus Flats Road, Olancha

    The town of Perfection, with Walter Chang's Market, the street and the houses, was built as a set in the open desert off Cactus Flats Road outside Olancha and torn down after the 1989 shoot. Nothing stands today: only faint dirt tracks of the old layout show in aerial imagery. It is remote, unpaved desert with no services, and the land ownership is not confirmed, so stay on the public road.

    See it on the map · Source: scabbyhorror.blogspot.com
