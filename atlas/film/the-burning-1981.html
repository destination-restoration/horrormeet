Filming locations

Where was The Burning (1981) filmed?

The Burning (1981) was filmed at Ransomville, New York and Camp Scouthaven, Freedom, New York. 2 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Ransomville, New York

    Camp Stonewood, where Cropsy works through the campers, was the former Camp Stonehaven outside this village west of Buffalo. The land is now the Stonehaven Nature Preserve.

    See it on the map · Source: fangoria.com

  • Camp Scouthaven, Freedom, New York

    The second camp used in the film, in the hills south of Buffalo. It is still an operating Scout camp.

    See it on the map · Source: fangoria.com
