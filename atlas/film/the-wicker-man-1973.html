Filming locations

Where was The Wicker Man (1973) filmed?

6 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Burrow Head

    The clifftop where the wicker man burns and Sergeant Howie screams. Two charred stumps of the second effigy stood in this field for decades. Bring a coat.

  • Anwoth Old Kirk

    The deconsecrated ruin and graveyard where Howie finds Rowan's grave with a hare in the coffin, plus the maypole dance nearby. Still roofless, still unsettling.

  • Culzean Castle

    Christopher Lee's Lord Summerisle lives here, at least on the outside. Robert Adam's clifftop castle on the Ayrshire coast, now National Trust for Scotland.

  • St Ninian's Cave

    The sea cave where Howie finally finds Rowan, right before he understands the trap. A genuine early Christian pilgrimage site, reached on foot down a wooded glen.

  • Plockton Harbour

    Howie's seaplane taxis in to Summerisle right here on Loch Carron, then he walks the seafront asking about a missing girl. Filmed over four days in 1972 and barely changed.

  • Ellangowan Hotel, Creetown

    The interior of the Green Man Inn, where Britt Ekland's Willow sings and the locals close ranks on Sergeant Howie. Still a hotel on Creetown's St John Street.
