Filming locations

Where was Silent Hill (2006) filmed?

6 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Colborne Street, Brantford

    Silent Hill's Main Street, with the ash falling. The whole south side of this Brantford block has since been demolished.

  • Lister Block, Hamilton

    The exterior of the Grand Hotel. The 1923 building was derelict during filming and has since been restored as offices.

  • Scottish Rite Club, Hamilton

    The Queen Street entrance plays the Toluca County Orphanage, and the King Street West side becomes the Silent Hill church of Christabella's congregation.

  • 53 Dalhousie Street, Brantford

    Braham's Archives, where Rose reads the town's history, on the corner of Queen Street. It is now a coffee shop.

  • Alma College site, St Thomas

    Midwich Elementary School, with the ruined classrooms and the boiler room, was the abandoned Alma College. The building burned down in 2008 and only ruins and the gates remain.

  • Devil's Punch Bowl, Stoney Creek

    The waterfall in the prologue that Sharon sleepwalks toward. It is a conservation area with a lookout over Hamilton.
