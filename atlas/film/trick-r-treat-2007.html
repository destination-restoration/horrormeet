Filming locations

Where was Trick 'r Treat (2007) filmed?

4 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Emma and Henry's house, 3742 West 36th Avenue

    The house where the jack-o'-lantern rule gets broken in the opening story, on a Vancouver street that supplied four of the film's homes. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE.

  • Wilkins house, 3707 West 36th Avenue

    Principal Wilkins's house, complete with the garden he should not be digging in, one block of West 36th Avenue from the others. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE.

  • Rhonda's house, 3775 West 36th Avenue

    Home of Rhonda, the girl the other kids take to the quarry, on the same Vancouver block as the rest of Warren Valley. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE.

  • Kreeg house, 3725 West 36th Avenue

    The house where Mr Kreeg meets Sam in the final story, with the porch and the dog. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE.
