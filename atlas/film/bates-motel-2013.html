Where was Bates Motel (2013) filmed?

Bates Motel (2013) was filmed at Aldergrove, British Columbia, Fort Langley Community Hall, Fort Langley, British Columbia, Horseshoe Bay Park, West Vancouver, British Columbia, Bay Market, Horseshoe Bay, West Vancouver, British Columbia and 8 more. 12 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Aldergrove, British Columbia

    The replica Psycho motel and the Bates house were built from scratch on farmland on 272 Street in this Langley community and used for all five seasons. Production tore the house down the day after the final scene was shot in January 2017 and the rest of the set was demolished that February, so there is nothing left to visit.

    See it on the map · Source: aldergrovestar.com

  • Fort Langley Community Hall, Fort Langley, British Columbia

    The production dressed this hall as the White Pine Bay Woodchuck Festival for an overnight shoot in October 2012, used in season 1 for Norma's date with Deputy Shelby. It is a working community hall at 9167 Glover Road that is rented for events. The exterior can be seen from the street at any time.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Horseshoe Bay Park, West Vancouver, British Columbia

    The waterfront park was dressed as White Pine Bay for crowd scenes in season 2 episode 4, and Dylan's talk with his uncle Caleb was filmed the same day on a dock at the marina beside it. Horseshoe Bay doubled for the town from the first season on. It is a public park next to the ferry terminal and free to visit.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Bay Market, Horseshoe Bay, West Vancouver, British Columbia

    For season 1 episode 7 the crew turned this corner grocery into Bay Market and Hardware, where Norman and Dylan buy supplies at a fall hardware sale and run into Bradley outside. The shop sits at 6414 Bruce Street on the corner with Royal Avenue. It is a business on a public street, so the storefront is easy to see from the sidewalk.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Horseshoe Bay Motel, West Vancouver, British Columbia

    This motel played The King's Motel in season 2 episode 4, where Norma sits in her Mercedes staring up at her brother Caleb's room and Norman later goes in to confront him. It is a real motel at 6588 Royal Avenue. A condo redevelopment has been proposed for the site, so check that it is still standing, and view it from the street unless you are a guest.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Troll's Restaurant, Horseshoe Bay, West Vancouver, British Columbia

    Romero and Dylan's meeting about how dangerous Norman has become, in the season 4 episode Forever, was shot here in long takes in March 2016. Troll's is a restaurant at 6408 Bay Street and open to the public. A hotel redevelopment of this corner has been approved, so check that it is still trading before you go.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Steveston Fisherman's Wharf, Richmond, British Columbia

    In November 2012 the show filmed on the Steveston docks and a fishing boat for season 1 episode 4, where Sheriff Romero and Deputy Shelby are called out after a fisherman finds a hand in his net. White Pine Bay County Sheriff cars were parked on the docks for the shoot. The wharf is a public working dock that visitors can walk for free.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site, Steveston, Richmond, British Columbia

    A regular location for the series. In season 2 episode 9 Norma runs down the walkway to a locked door and screams to see Dylan, a scene Vera Farmiga repeated at least a dozen times here in November 2013. It is a national historic site and public waterfront park at 5180 Westwater Drive, open to visitors.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Dave's Fish and Chips, Moncton Street, Steveston, Richmond, British Columbia

    In November 2014 a block of Moncton Street was turned into White Pine Bay for season 3, with Norma parking her green Mercedes outside Dave's Fish and Chips while Caleb unloads his jeep a little to the east. The restaurant at 3460 Moncton Street is a business open to the public. The same block is better known as Storybrooke in Once Upon a Time.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • Seycove Secondary School, North Vancouver, British Columbia

    Seycove Secondary played Norman's high school in the series. It is a working public school at 1204 Caledonia Avenue in the Deep Cove area. It is not open to visitors, so view the exterior from the street and stay off the grounds during school hours.

    See it on the map · Source: screenrant.com

  • Vancouver Art Gallery, Vancouver, British Columbia

    Norman's murder trial in the final season, in the episode that aired April 17, 2017, was filmed in the old law courtroom inside this former courthouse. The building is now the Vancouver Art Gallery at 750 Hornby Street. The exterior is free to see at any time, and gallery admission applies inside.

    See it on the map · Source: hollywoodnorthbuzz.com

  • 2400 Motel, Kingsway, Vancouver, British Columbia

    The 2400 Motel appears in season 1 episode 8, according to a location blogger who matched it against screenshots from the episode. It is a working motor court at 2400 Kingsway that has been used by many other productions. The city plans to redevelop the site, so check that it is still standing, and view it from the street unless you are a guest.

    See it on the map · Source: movielocationsandmore.blogspot.com
