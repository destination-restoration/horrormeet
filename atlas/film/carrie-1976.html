Filming locations

Where was Carrie (1976) filmed?

Carrie (1976) was filmed at Bates High School Gym, Bates High Track and Fields and Farmer John's Pig Farm. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Bates High School Gym

    The old Pier Avenue Junior High, now the Hermosa Beach Community Center, was Bates High and the prom gym where the pig blood drops and Carrie burns it all down.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Bates High Track and Fields

    Pali High's playing fields stood in for Bates High gym class, where Miss Collins drills the girls as punishment for the shower scene. Active public school, view from the street.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Farmer John's Pig Farm

    The Vernon slaughterhouse behind the famous painted pig murals is where Billy and Chris fill the bucket. The murals still wrap the block even though the plant has closed.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
