Where was Mimic (1997) filmed?

Mimic (1997) was filmed at R.C. Harris Water Treatment Plant, Toronto, Osgoode Hall Great Library, Toronto, Knox College Cloisters, Toronto, Wycliffe College, Toronto and 2 more. 6 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • R.C. Harris Water Treatment Plant, Toronto

    The Art Deco waterworks on the Lake Ontario shore opens the film as the strange ward of glowing hospital beds. The plant still operates and the grounds are open to the public.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Osgoode Hall Great Library, Toronto

    Dr Mann announces the Judas breed in this 19th century law library. Osgoode Hall is a working courthouse and law society.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Knox College Cloisters, Toronto

    The Gothic interior of the Museum of Natural History where Susan Tyler collects insect specimens from the neighbourhood children. Knox is a theological college on the University of Toronto campus.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Wycliffe College, Toronto

    Leonard Hall's faux medieval library is where Tyler and Gates realise the Judas breed is not sterile.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Wellington Destructor, Toronto

    The disused 1920s rubbish incinerator is the water treatment plant where Jeremy shows off the body of a giant bug. It stood empty for decades and is being redeveloped.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Lower Bay station, Toronto

    Delancey Street Station, where Susan Tyler asks a giant insect for the time, is the abandoned Lower Bay platform under Bay station. It was open for only six months in 1966 and is used mainly for filming.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
