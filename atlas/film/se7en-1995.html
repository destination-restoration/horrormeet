Filming locations

Where was Se7en (1995) filmed?

11 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • 746 South San Pedro Street, Los Angeles

    The rainy shopfronts of the opening murder, where Somerset and Mills first meet. The distinctive iron grillwork on the doors is unchanged.

  • 720 Beacon Avenue, Los Angeles

    The roach infested house of the Gluttony victim, in Westlake east of MacArthur Park. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE and far less grim than the film makes it look.

  • Pacific Electric Building, Los Angeles

    The third floor supplied the police station interiors, the same floor used in L.A. Confidential. The building is now lofts.

  • Biscuit Company Lofts, Los Angeles

    The Fourteenth Precinct exterior was the old National Biscuit Company headquarters at 673 Mateo Street. It became apartments in 2006 and was readdressed as 1850 Industrial Street.

  • Rosslyn Hotel, Los Angeles

    The station lobby where John Doe walks in covered in blood and surrenders. The long derelict hotel is now the Rosslyn Lofts.

  • 650 South Spring Street, Los Angeles

    The gloomy library where Somerset reads up on the seven deadly sins is the disused Bank of America building, also the New York vault in Marathon Man.

  • Pan American Lofts, Los Angeles

    Where the barely alive Sloth victim is found, across the street from the Bradbury Building. The old Giant Penny sign and canopy were removed in the conversion to lofts.

  • Alexandria Hotel, Los Angeles

    John Doe's apartment block, including the elaborate Mezzanine Ballroom the detectives run through. Once a luxury hotel, then a welfare hotel, it is now mixed residential housing.

  • Chester Williams Building, Los Angeles

    Somerset emerges from this building opposite the Alexandria during the rooftop chase. It was also Karen's apartment block in Blade and the ledge in Inception.

  • 117 East 6th Street, Los Angeles

    The barbershop where the detectives wait for the library records. It is still in business, now as a beauty salon.

  • West Avenue I at 110th Street West, Lancaster

    The field of high tension power lines in the Mojave where the box arrives and the film ends. It is near featureless desert on a grid of streets that were never built.
