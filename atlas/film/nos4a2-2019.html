Filming locations

Where was NOS4A2 (2019) filmed?

NOS4A2 (2019) was filmed at Providence Athenaeum, Goddard Memorial State Park, Warwick, Warren Town Hall, Rhode Island and West Warwick High School. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Providence Athenaeum

    The 1838 subscription library on Benefit Street was used for a library sequence. Poe courted Sarah Helen Whitman in this building, and it is still a working library.

    See it on the map · Source: valleybreeze.com

  • Goddard Memorial State Park, Warwick

    Charlie Manx's Christmasland sequences were shot in this state park on Greenwich Bay. It is open to the public.

    See it on the map · Source: valleybreeze.com

  • Warren Town Hall, Rhode Island

    Stood in for the fictional town of Here, Iowa. It is a working municipal building.

    See it on the map · Source: valleybreeze.com

  • West Warwick High School

    Played Vic McQueen's William White Memorial High School. It is an operating public high school.

    See it on the map · Source: valleybreeze.com
