Filming locations

Where was Shaun of the Dead (2004) filmed?

3 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Shaun's flat, 83 Nelson Road

    The Crouch End house Shaun shares with Pete and Ed, and the front garden where he steps over the fence. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE, so look from the pavement only.

  • Corner shop, 96 Weston Park

    The shop Shaun walks to for a Cornetto while failing to notice the apocalypse. It was Weston Park Food in the film and now trades as a Londis convenience store.

  • The Winchester, 39 Monson Road

    The Duke of Albany in New Cross Gate supplied the exterior of the Winchester Tavern, with interiors built at Ealing Studios. The pub closed and the building has been converted into flats.
