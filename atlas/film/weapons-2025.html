Where was Weapons (2025) filmed?

Weapons (2025) was filmed at Brockett Elementary School, Tucker, Alex's house, 4095 Brockett Creek Court, USA Package, Conyers and Trueco service station, Covington. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Brockett Elementary School, Tucker

    Maybrook Elementary, the school where seventeen children walk out of their beds at 2.17am, is this school on Brockett Road northeast of Atlanta. It is a working public school.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Alex's house, 4095 Brockett Creek Court

    Home of the one child who did not disappear, in a cul-de-sac that backs onto the school. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • USA Package, Conyers

    The liquor store Justine keeps returning to, on West Avenue in Conyers about 24 miles east of Atlanta. It is an operating store.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Trueco service station, Covington

    The gas station where Justine is confronted by a furious parent, a working BP on Pace Street in Covington. The forecourt is a public business, so keep out of the way.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com
