Filming locations

Where was Prom Night (1980) filmed?

Prom Night (1980) was filmed at Don Mills Collegiate Institute, Toronto and St James Cemetery, Toronto. 2 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Don Mills Collegiate Institute, Toronto

    Alexander Hamilton High, where the prom and the killings happen. The production took over the school's woodworking shop, and it is still a working Toronto school.

    See it on the map · Source: fast-rewind.com

  • St James Cemetery, Toronto

    The cemetery scenes were shot at this 1844 burial ground on Parliament Street, the oldest still operating cemetery in Toronto.

    See it on the map · Source: fast-rewind.com
