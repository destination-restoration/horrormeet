Filming locations

Where was Fright Night (1985) filmed?

2 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Walt Disney Studios, Burbank

    The Brewster and Dandrige houses were built as facades on the Disney lot's Residential Street set so the crew could get crane shots down the block. That set was torn down in 1991 to make room for soundstages, so nothing of Fright Night's street survives.

  • The Culver Studios, Culver City

    Known as Laird International Studios when Fright Night shot there, this lot held the house interiors and duplicate exteriors built by production designer John De Cuir Jr. The studio is still operating under its original Culver Studios name.
