Where was Jacob's Ladder (1990) filmed?

Jacob's Ladder (1990) was filmed at Bergen Street station, Brooklyn, Seaview Hospital, Staten Island and Essex County Veterans Courthouse, Newark. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Bergen Street station, Brooklyn

    The nightmarish subway sequence was shot on the abandoned lower level of this IND Culver Line station, which had to be re-tiled to look in service. The lower platforms are still closed to the public.

    See it on the map · Source: en.wikipedia.org

  • Seaview Hospital, Staten Island

    Hospital corridors were shot at the former Seaview tuberculosis hospital. Much of the complex is derelict and it is a designated landmark.

    See it on the map · Source: en.wikipedia.org

  • Essex County Veterans Courthouse, Newark

    Used for the courthouse scenes during the New York area shoot that began in September 1989. It is a working county courthouse.

    See it on the map · Source: en.wikipedia.org
