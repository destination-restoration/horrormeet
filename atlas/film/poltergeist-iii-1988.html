Where was Poltergeist III (1988) filmed?

Poltergeist III (1988) was filmed at 875 North Michigan Avenue, Chicago, Water Tower Place, Chicago and 1435 North Astor Street, Chicago. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • 875 North Michigan Avenue, Chicago

    The black cross-braced tower where Carol Anne stays with her aunt and uncle, and where the mirrors turn against everyone, is the former John Hancock Center. It still stands on the Magnificent Mile and the observation deck is open as 360 Chicago.

    See it on the map · Source: en.wikipedia.org

  • Water Tower Place, Chicago

    The crossing escalators in the mall sequence were shot at Water Tower Place, directly across Michigan Avenue from the Hancock tower. The mall is still open, although many of its upper floors have emptied out.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com

  • 1435 North Astor Street, Chicago

    Carol Anne's school was shot at this building on the Gold Coast's Astor Street, a block of protected nineteenth-century mansions. The block is PRIVATE property: walk the sidewalk and do not enter any gate.

    See it on the map · Source: set-jetter.com
