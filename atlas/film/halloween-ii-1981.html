Filming locations

Where was Halloween II (1981) filmed?

3 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Sepulveda Ambulatory Care Center

    The exterior of Haddonfield Memorial Hospital, where the ambulance rolls in with a sedated Laurie. Rob Zombie later shot the same complex as Smith's Grove.

  • Pasadena Community Hospital

    Most of Haddonfield Memorial's interiors, including the reception desk, guard station, basement and generator rooms Michael stalks through all night.

  • Alice's House

    The Sierra Madre house where Michael claims Alice, his first kill after slipping away from the Myers place on Halloween night. Private residence, admire from the sidewalk.
