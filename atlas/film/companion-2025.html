Where was Companion (2025) filmed?

Companion (2025) was filmed at Durland Scout Reservation, Putnam Valley. 1 real location below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open it on the live map

  • Durland Scout Reservation, Putnam Valley

    The lakeside scenes were shot at this Scout camp on Clear Lake in Putnam County, because the house used for the film had no lake of its own. The water was extended digitally in post.

    See it on the map · Source: timesunion.com
