Filming locations

Where was Doctor Sleep (2019) filmed?

Doctor Sleep (2019) was filmed at Plaza Theatre, Abra Stone's House and Frazier Town Square. 3 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Plaza Theatre

    Atlanta's oldest operating cinema appears as Dan Torrance moves through his new town. Still running horror programming, which feels about right.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Abra Stone's House

    The Ansley Park stone house where young Abra writes MURDER on her bedroom wall and starts pulling the True Knot toward her. Private residence.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com

  • Frazier Town Square

    Covington's square plays Frazier, New Hampshire, where Dan steps off the bus and tries to get sober. The mountains behind it are CGI.

    See it on the map · Source: thennowmovielocations.com
