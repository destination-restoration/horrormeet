Filming locations

Where was Stranger Things filmed?

8 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • The Byers House

    The real Hawkins house where Joyce tore down the walls and strung up the Christmas-light alphabet in Season 1. Private residence the owners now rent, so admire it from the road.

  • Starcourt Mall / Gwinnett Place Mall

    The dying Georgia mall the production restored to 1985 glory as Starcourt for Season 3, complete with Scoops Ahoy and the Russian base underneath.

  • Hawkins Public Library

    The Butts County Courthouse doubles as the Hawkins Public Library, where the kids dig through microfilm on Eleven and the Hawkins Lab. Roughly seven Hawkins locations sit within a walk.

  • Hawkins Radio Shack

    The Jackson storefront dressed as Hawkins' Radio Shack, now a fan shop and the starting point for the town's Stranger Things walking tours.

  • The Creel House

    Vecna's Victorian nightmare in Season 4 is the 1882 Claremont House in Rome, all mansard roof and bad intentions. Private residence, shoot it from the public sidewalk.

  • The Wheeler House

    Mike and Nancy's split-level, where the basement D&D campaign kicked off the whole series. Private home on a quiet East Point street.

  • The Palace Arcade

    Downtown Douglasville's storefront became the Palace Arcade, home of Dig Dug high scores and MADMAX taking the crown in Season 2.

  • Hawkins National Laboratory

    The shuttered Georgia Mental Health Institute on Emory's Briarcliff campus played the Department of Energy lab in Seasons 1 and 2. That long brick facade is where Eleven escaped from.
