Filming locations

Where was Nope (2022) filmed?

3 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Jupiter's Claim at Universal Studios

    Ricky Park's western theme park was built for the shoot, then dismantled and rebuilt permanently at Universal Studios Hollywood. You can walk the actual Star Lasso grandstand on the studio tour.

  • Firestone Ranch, Agua Dulce

    The Haywood Hollywood Horses ranch, where OJ watches the cloud that never moves. The house was a set and came down after the shoot, but the ridgeline is unmistakable.

  • The Old Fry's Electronics, Burbank

    The big-box store where Angel sells OJ and Em the surveillance cameras that will maybe catch Jean Jacket on film. Closed since 2021, but the building still stands.
