Filming locations

Where was Interview with the Vampire (1994) filmed?

4 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Oak Alley Plantation

    Louis' Pointe du Lac estate, with that quarter-mile tunnel of 300-year-old live oaks Brad Pitt and Tom Cruise stalk beneath. Open to visitors daily.

  • Destrehan Plantation

    The 1787 house on River Road that supplied the plantation interiors, including where Lestat turns Louis. Oldest documented plantation home left in the lower Mississippi Valley.

  • Palais Garnier

    The grand staircase and gilded lobby standing in for the Parisian hotel where Louis and Claudia arrive in Europe. Vampires deserve this much marble.

  • Phoenix Cinema, East Finchley

    The auditorium used for the scene where Louis watches a sunrise on screen for the first time in centuries. Still a working cinema, and one of Britain's oldest.
