Filming locations

Where was Candyman (2021) filmed?

3 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Cabrini-Green Row Houses

    The 1942 row houses are the last standing piece of Cabrini-Green, and Nia DaCosta filmed inside them. The 1992 original shot its stairwells in the high-rises that once stood just north.

  • Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago

    Anthony's gallery world was shot inside the MCA, the first feature film ever allowed to film in the museum. You can walk straight in during opening hours.

  • Marina City

    The corncob towers on the river are Brianna and Anthony's apartment complex, and the director's favorite location in the whole film. Gentrified Chicago staring straight at Cabrini-Green.
