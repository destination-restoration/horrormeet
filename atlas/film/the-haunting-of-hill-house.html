Filming locations

Where was The Haunting of Hill House filmed?

The Haunting of Hill House was filmed at Bisham Manor and The Whitlock Inn. 2 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Bisham Manor

    The 1920s Tudor estate near West Point Lake that played every exterior of Hill House, including the red room windows. Now a private residence, view from the road.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com

  • The Whitlock Inn

    Marietta's historic event house stood in for the Harris Funeral Home in Episode 6, the bottle episode built around Nell's wake.

    See it on the map · Source: atlasofwonders.com
