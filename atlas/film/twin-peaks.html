Filming locations

Where was Twin Peaks filmed?

4 locations on the HorrorMeet Atlas.

  • Twede's Cafe / The Double R Diner

    The Double R, home of damn fine coffee and cherry pie, has been serving both since 1990 and again in The Return. Still a working diner on North Bend's main drag.

  • Salish Lodge / The Great Northern

    Agent Cooper's home base, perched right on the lip of Snoqualmie Falls and seen in the opening credits of every episode. The exterior is essentially unchanged.

  • Twin Peaks Sheriff's Department

    The old Weyerhaeuser mill office played both the Packard Sawmill and the Sheriff's Department where Cooper, Truman and Lucy worked the Laura Palmer case. Now a rally driving school.

  • The Roadhouse, Fall City

    The exterior of the Roadhouse and Bang Bang Bar, where Julee Cruise sang and half the town's secrets got traded. Operating since 1916 and still pouring.
