Filming locations

Where was Twin Peaks filmed?

Twin Peaks was filmed at Twede's Cafe / The Double R Diner, Salish Lodge / The Great Northern, Twin Peaks Sheriff's Department and The Roadhouse, Fall City. 4 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Twede's Cafe / The Double R Diner

    The Double R, home of damn fine coffee and cherry pie, has been serving both since 1990 and again in The Return. Still a working diner on North Bend's main drag.

    See it on the map · Source: twedescafe.com

  • Salish Lodge / The Great Northern

    Agent Cooper's home base, perched right on the lip of Snoqualmie Falls and seen in the opening credits of every episode. The exterior is essentially unchanged.

    See it on the map · Source: twinpeaksblog.com

  • Twin Peaks Sheriff's Department

    The old Weyerhaeuser mill office played both the Packard Sawmill and the Sheriff's Department where Cooper, Truman and Lucy worked the Laura Palmer case. Now a rally driving school.

    See it on the map · Source: valleyrecord.com

  • The Roadhouse, Fall City

    The exterior of the Roadhouse and Bang Bang Bar, where Julee Cruise sang and half the town's secrets got traded. Operating since 1916 and still pouring.

    See it on the map · Source: valleyrecord.com

Quick answers

Where was Twin Peaks filmed?
Twin Peaks was filmed at Twede's Cafe / The Double R Diner, Salish Lodge / The Great Northern, Twin Peaks Sheriff's Department and The Roadhouse, Fall City. Every location on this page is a real place with its spot on the HorrorMeet Atlas map.

Can you visit the Twin Peaks filming locations?
Many of them, yes. Each location above says whether it is a public place, a private home or no longer standing. Private homes are for looking at from the sidewalk only: never knock, never step onto the property.
