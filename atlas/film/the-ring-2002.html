Where was The Ring (2002) filmed?

The Ring (2002) was filmed at Katie's house, 413 South McCadden Place, Metropolitan State Hospital, Norwalk, Hoose Library, Mudd Hall, USC, Harbor Steps Apartments, Seattle and 5 more. 9 real locations below, each with what was shot there and its place on the map.

Open them on the live map

  • Katie's house, 413 South McCadden Place

    The Hancock Park house where Katie admits she has already watched the tape, and where she dies in the closet. It is a PRIVATE RESIDENCE in Los Angeles, standing in for suburban Seattle.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Metropolitan State Hospital, Norwalk

    The psychiatric hospital where Rachel interviews the traumatised Becca. It is a working California state hospital and not open to visitors.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Hoose Library, Mudd Hall, USC

    Rachel's lighthouse research scene was shot in the philosophy library inside Mudd Hall on the USC campus. The cloistered brick building is one of the most filmed corners of the university.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Harbor Steps Apartments, Seattle

    Rachel and Aidan's apartment building on Western Avenue, at the top of the Harbor Steps above the waterfront. It is a private residential complex, though the public stairway beside it is open.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Photo shop, 1923 Fifth Avenue, Seattle

    The shop beneath the Seattle Monorail track where Rachel collects Katie's distorted photographs. The storefront has changed hands since filming.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Shelter Mountain Inn, Camp Firwood

    The cabins where the cursed tape is found were shot at Camp Firwood on the shore of Lake Whatcom near Bellingham. It operates as a Christian summer camp and is private property.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Deception Pass Bridge

    Rachel and Noah drive north over this 1935 steel arch bridge between Whidbey and Fidalgo islands on the way to the lighthouse. There is parking at either end and a walkway across.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Yaquina Head Lighthouse

    The Moesko Island Lighthouse of the film is Oregon's tallest lighthouse, north of Newport. It is still an active light, with tours run at the Yaquina Head Outstanding Natural Area.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

  • Emerald Glen Farm, Monroe

    The Morgan horse ranch, where Rachel confronts Richard Morgan and finds the well, is a working farm on Yeager Road off Highway 2. It is PRIVATE PROPERTY.

    See it on the map · Source: movie-locations.com

Can you visit the The Ring (2002) filming locations?
