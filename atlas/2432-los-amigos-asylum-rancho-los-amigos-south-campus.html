Los Amigos Asylum (Rancho Los Amigos South Campus)

The abandoned south campus of Rancho Los Amigos, known locally as the Downey asylum or Hollydale Mental Hospital. It opened in 1888 as the Los Angeles County Poor Farm, became a long-term hospital in 1907, took the name Rancho Los Amigos in 1932, and was boarded up in 1988. Decades of urban legends say former patients still walk the halls. Bubba Ho-Tep (2002) filmed in one of its empty buildings. The county approved demolition of nearly all of its 103 structures in 2020; the water tower, the giant Moreton Bay fig and a few buildings were to be kept. Fenced county property: look from outside the fence, never go in.
