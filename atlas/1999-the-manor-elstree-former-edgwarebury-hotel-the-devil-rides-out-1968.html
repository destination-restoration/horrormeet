Filming location · The Devil Rides Out (1968)

The Manor Elstree, former Edgwarebury Hotel

The Eatons' country house, where the characters sit out the night inside the chalk circle, was the Edgwarebury Hotel at Elstree. The building is now a hotel called The Manor Elstree.
