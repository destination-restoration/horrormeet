Château de Trécesson

Listed among reportedly haunted places. The Château de Trécesson (Breton: Kastell Treseson) is a medieval castle in the Brittany region of France. It is located in the commune of Campénéac near the Paimpont forest and on the edge of the military camp of Coëtquidan. It is a private property.
