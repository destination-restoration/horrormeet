Filming location · American Horror Story: Asylum

Old Orange County Courthouse / Briarcliff Manor

The 1901 Romanesque courthouse in Santa Ana played the exterior of Briarcliff Manor asylum, with a whole extra floor added digitally; Briarcliff's interiors were studio sets. It popped up again in Freak Show's Orphans.
