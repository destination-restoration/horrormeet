New Fountain, Texas

A ghost town in South Texas. New Fountain is a ghost town established in 1846. It is located 1.8 miles (2.9 km) west of Quihi and 5.6 miles (9.0 km) east-northeast of Hondo in Medina County in the U.S. state of Texas.
