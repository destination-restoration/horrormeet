Filming location · Bates Motel (2013)

Seycove Secondary School, North Vancouver, British Columbia

Seycove Secondary played Norman's high school in the series. It is a working public school at 1204 Caledonia Avenue in the Deep Cove area. It is not open to visitors, so view the exterior from the street and stay off the grounds during school hours.
