Filming location · Zombi 2 (1979)

TWA Flight Center, JFK Airport

The airport lounge scenes were shot inside Eero Saarinen's 1962 TWA terminal at Kennedy Airport. The building sat empty for years and reopened in 2019 as the TWA Hotel, so the swooping lobby can now be walked through.
