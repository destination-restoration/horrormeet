Filming location · The Ward (2010)

Eastern State Hospital, Medical Lake, Washington

Carpenter shot the North Bend Psychiatric Hospital scenes inside this state psychiatric facility west of Spokane, using older wards that were out of service. It remains an active state hospital and is not open to visitors.
