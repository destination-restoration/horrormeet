Filming location · The Wailing (2016)

Maewoldae Falls, Geunnam-myeon, Cheorwon

The stranger's ritual bathing under a waterfall was filmed here in mid September, after the producers could not find a deep enough waterfall in the south of the country. The falls sit on the slopes of Bokgyesan and are one of Cheorwon's listed scenic sights. It is a public hiking destination, open at all times, reached by a rough mountain trail.
