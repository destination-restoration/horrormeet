Filming location · Raw (2016)

Faculty of Veterinary Medicine, University of Liege

Julia Ducournau shot her cannibal coming of age story on this 1970s concrete veterinary campus at Sart Tilman, having found no French vet school with the American style layout she wanted. It is a working university faculty, so visitors should keep to public areas.
