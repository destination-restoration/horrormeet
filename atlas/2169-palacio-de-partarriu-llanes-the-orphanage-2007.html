Filming location · The Orphanage (2007)

Palacio de Partarriu, Llanes

This 19th century mansion outside Llanes in Asturias played the orphanage for four weeks of shooting before the production moved to sound stages in Barcelona. It is a PRIVATE HOUSE and appears on the town's cinema walking route, to be viewed from the road.
