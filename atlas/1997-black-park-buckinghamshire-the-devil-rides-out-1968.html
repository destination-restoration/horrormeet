Filming location · The Devil Rides Out (1968)

Black Park, Buckinghamshire

The clearing where the Satanists summon the Goat of Mendes was shot in Black Park, the pine woodland next to Pinewood Studios that Hammer used again and again. It is a country park with free public access and a car park.
