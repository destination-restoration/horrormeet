Tai Tung (restaurant)

Named among Washington's reportedly haunted places. Tai Tung (traditional Chinese: 大同飯店; simplified Chinese: 大同饭店) is the oldest surviving Chinese restaurant in the International District of Seattle. It was opened in 1935 by an immigrant from Hong Kong.
