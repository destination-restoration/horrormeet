Changi Hospital

Listed among reportedly haunted places. Changi Hospital is an abandoned general hospital located in Changi, Singapore. Its closure came with the merging with the former Toa Payoh Hospital and was renamed as the Changi General Hospital, which relocated new operations to nearby Simei, not far from Changi. It began winding down activities in February 1997 and was completely abandoned by December.
