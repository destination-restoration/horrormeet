Filming location · The Beyond (1981)

Fairview-Riverside State Park, Madisonville

The Victorian Otis House inside this Louisiana state park was the Seven Doors Hotel, the building sitting on one of the gateways to hell. The park is open to visitors and the house can be toured, with the hotel interiors having been built on a Rome sound stage.
