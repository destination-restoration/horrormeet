Joliet Correctional Center

Named among Illinois's reportedly haunted places. Joliet Correctional Center (originally known as Illinois State Penitentiary, colloquially as Joliet Prison, Joliet Penitentiary, the Old Joliet Prison, and the Collins Street Prison) is a former prison in Joliet, Illinois, United States, which operated from 1858 to 2002. Numerous films and television productions have used the prison as a setting or filming location.
