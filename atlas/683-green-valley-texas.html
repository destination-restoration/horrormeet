Green Valley, Texas

A ghost town in North Texas. Green Valley, previously called Toll Town, is a ghost town in northeast Denton County in the state of Texas in the United States of America. The ghost town is about 8 miles away from the city of Denton and rests near the Elm Fork in the Trinity River.
