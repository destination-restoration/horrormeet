Filming location · The Lighthouse (2019)

Cape Forchu Lightstation

Eggers built a 70-foot working lighthouse tower on this windswept Nova Scotia cape for Dafoe and Pattinson to lose their minds in. The real light and its brutal weather are open to visitors.
