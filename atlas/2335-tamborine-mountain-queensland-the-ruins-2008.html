Filming location · The Ruins (2008)

Tamborine Mountain, Queensland

Three quarters of a Mayan pyramid, big enough for the cast to climb, was built on this Gold Coast hinterland plateau over about seven weeks. The set is long gone and the mountain is a rainforest and winery area.
