Filming location · Trick 'r Treat (2007)

Delta Street, Ladner Village, Delta

Delta Street in historic Ladner Village stood in for the downtown streets of Warren Valley, Ohio. Today it is a working village main street of shops and restaurants. It is a public street and visitors can walk it freely.
