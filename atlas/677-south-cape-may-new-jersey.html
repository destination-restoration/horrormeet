South Cape May, New Jersey

A ghost town in New Jersey. South Cape May was a borough that existed in Cape May County, in the U.S. state of New Jersey, from 1894 to 1945. The borough was established on August 27, 1894, from portions of Lower Township, based on the results of a referendum held six days earlier.
