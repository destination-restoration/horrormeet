Filming location · Frankenstein (1931)

Malibou Lake

The lakeside where the Monster meets little Maria and drowns her was shot at Malibou Lake in the Santa Monica Mountains. The lake itself sits inside a gated private community, so it can only be glimpsed from Mulholland Highway.
