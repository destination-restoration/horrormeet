Belkofski, Alaska

A ghost town in Alaska. Belkofski (Aleut: Taxtamax̂; Russian: Белкофский) is an abandoned unincorporated community and Alaska Native Village Statistical Area (ANVSA) in Aleutians East Borough, Alaska. It has been uninhabited since the 1980s, reporting a population of 0 in 1990, 2000 and 2010.
