Ceramic, North Carolina

A ghost town in North Carolina. Ceramic is a ghost town in Stokes County, North Carolina, United States, approximately ten miles southeast of the county seat of Danbury, near Belews Lake. Ceramic as a community ceased to exist at least 6 decades ago.
