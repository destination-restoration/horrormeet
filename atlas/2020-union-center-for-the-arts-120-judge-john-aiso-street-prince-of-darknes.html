Filming location · Prince of Darkness (1987)

Union Center for the Arts, 120 Judge John Aiso Street

The Little Tokyo building that plays the abandoned church holding the cylinder of green liquid. Long the Japanese Union Church of Los Angeles, it is now the East West Players theatre and arts center.
