Filming location · Pumpkinhead (1988)

Mentryville, Pico Canyon

The Harley grocery gate and the surrounding backroad country were shot at this preserved 1890s oil boomtown in Pico Canyon above Santa Clarita. Mentryville is now a park site with the Felton schoolhouse and the Mentry house still standing.
