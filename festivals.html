THE CIRCUIT
The best places on earth to show a horror film · ranked · updated September 2026

Made a horror film? This is where it should play. Our editorial ranking of the festivals that matter for independent horror: judged on what they do for FILMMAKERS: discovery, acquisitions, press, audience, and how they treat you when you're nobody yet. Disagree with the order? That's what the board is for.

Featured festivals · friends of the house, front of the line
★1

Horizon International Film Festival SUBMIT ↗

Yin Ranch, Vacaville, CA · September 25-27, 2026

Our first partner festival, and front of the line by right. Founded by horror director Minh Collins, whose credits include a Lionsgate release and whose 24 Collins Studios runs a full soundstage and studio lot in Northern California. Year one unfolds on the Yin Ranch estate outside Sacramento: a festival built by a filmmaker, for filmmakers. HorrorMeet members: go show up for the inaugural. Founders remember who came first.

inaugural year · northern california · all genres, horror welcome

The world tier · if you can only submit to five on earth
W1

Sitges Film Festival SITE ↗

Sitges, Spain · October

The Cannes of genre. Fifty-plus years, the biggest fantastic-film festival in the world. A Sitges laurel changes a film's life.

plays everything · world-class premieres · awards with weight
The festival that helped detonate [REC] in 2007. A Sitges prize follows a film forever.
W2

Fantasia International Film Festival SITE ↗

Montreal, Canada · July-August

North America's biggest genre event. Three weeks, deep programming, real acquisitions. The premiere target for serious indie horror on this continent.

deep genre programming · acquisitions · international
Skinamarink went from a Fantasia 2022 premiere to a Shudder deal and a $2M theatrical run, on a $15,000 budget. Also the fest that introduced North America to the Ringu/Ju-on wave.
W3

FrightFest SITE ↗

London, UK · August

The UK's dark heart. Five days, ferociously loyal audience, and distributors in the seats.

uk launchpad · hard horror welcome · distributor seats
Opened 2002 with the world premiere of Dog Soldiers and has been launching UK horror careers since.
W4

BIFAN (Bucheon International Fantastic Film Festival) SITE ↗

Bucheon, South Korea · June-July

Asia's genre giant, with an industry market attached. If your film travels, it travels here.

asian market attached · international breakouts
W5

Brussels International Fantastic Film Festival (BIFFF) SITE ↗

Brussels, Belgium · Spring

Forty-plus years of beautiful chaos. The rowdiest, most beloved audience in genre cinema.

everything goes · legendary rowdy audience
The United States · ranked top to bottom
1

Fantastic Fest SITE ↗

Austin, TX · September

The biggest genre festival in the US. Industry, press, buyers, and the Alamo Drafthouse machine. The hardest US laurel to get and the most valuable.

all genre · industry and buyers · premieres with press
Has world-premiered films as big as There Will Be Blood and Zombieland. When buyers fly in for genre, they fly here.
2

Screamfest LA SITE ↗

Los Angeles, CA · October · TCL Chinese

The grande dame of American horror-only festivals. Paranormal Activity premiered here. Public director pages for every alum, a YouTube channel that keeps promoting your short for years, and Hollywood in the room.

horror-only · all subgenres · hollywood in the room
Paranormal Activity world-premiered here in 2007: a $15,000 film that became the starter of a franchise grossing hundreds of millions. The reference case for what one festival screening can do.
3

Overlook Film Festival SITE ↗

New Orleans, LA · Spring

Prestige curation in the most haunted city in America. Smaller slate, huge taste; programmers and press treat an Overlook pick as a mark of quality.

elevated and prestige · tight curation · press magnet
4

Beyond Fest SITE ↗

Los Angeles, CA · Fall

The highest-attended genre event in the US. Skews toward big premieres and repertory, so it's a harder door for micro-indies, but the room is unmatched.

big premieres and repertory · biggest genre audience in the US
Harder door for micro-indies, but a slot here means playing to the largest genre crowds in the country.
5

Nightmares Film Festival SITE ↗

Columbus, OH · October

The filmmaker's festival. One of the best-reviewed events on all of FilmFreeway, a year-round community, and the alumni network that actually answers your emails.

all budgets · extreme-friendly · filmmaker community first
Among the best-reviewed festivals on all of FilmFreeway, year after year, by the filmmakers themselves.
6

Panic Fest SITE ↗

Kansas City, MO · April

Community-driven, press-heavy (Fangoria, Bloody Disgusting cover the lineup), with a virtual edition that extends your reach. Great first big fest for a new filmmaker.

crowd-pleasers and discoveries · strong press · hybrid virtual
Fangoria, Bloody Disgusting, and Dread Central cover the lineup annually: small-fest entry fee, big-fest headlines.
7

Popcorn Frights SITE ↗

Fort Lauderdale, FL · August

The Southeast's biggest horror event: 100+ films a year, aggressive world-premiere slate, and real love for first-timers.

premiere-hungry · crowd-pleasers · first-timer friendly
116 films and 19 world premieres in 2026 alone. They feed on new blood, in the good way.
8

Brooklyn Horror Film Festival SITE ↗

Brooklyn, NY · October

New York's horror address. Nitehawk cinemas, sharp curation, Variety coverage, and a slate that mixes buzz titles with true unknowns.

curated indie · art-horror welcome · nyc press
Variety covers the lineup; unknowns share the schedule with the year's buzz titles.
9

Chattanooga Film Festival SITE ↗

Chattanooga, TN · Spring

Genre-soaked and famously warm to filmmakers. The festival other festivals' programmers attend for fun.

weird southern genre · all shapes · beloved by filmmakers
10

Telluride Horror Show SITE ↗

Telluride, CO · October

Small, curated, prestigious. Skews toward films that already have heat, but a gorgeous notch in any belt.

prestige · curated · films with heat
11

FilmQuest SITE ↗

Provo, UT · October

Filmmaker-first culture: mixers, prep lists, huge nominee slates. Alumni rave about it the way people rave about summer camp.

fantasy horror scifi · huge nominee slates · networking heavy
12

Salem Horror Fest SITE ↗

Salem, MA · Spring

Horror in the witch city, with a strong indie and queer-horror community and the Romero Foundation fellowship attached.

indie and queer horror · community · the witch city itself
Hosts the George A. Romero Foundation fellowship.
13

Boston Underground Film Festival SITE ↗

Boston, MA · March

The weird one, in the best way. If your film is strange as well as scary, BUFF's audience was built for it.

weird and experimental · transgressive welcome
14

HollyShorts (Horror Blocks) SITE ↗

Hollywood, CA · August

Oscar-qualifying shorts festival with dedicated horror programming at the TCL. For shorts chasing award eligibility, this is the door.

shorts only · oscar-qualifying · industry town
An Oscar-qualifying laurel for a horror short is rare currency. This is where it gets minted.
15

Horrible Imaginings Film Festival SITE ↗

Santa Ana, CA

Nonprofit, panel-heavy, community-first. One of the most welcoming rooms in the genre for a first film.

community and panels · nonprofit · newcomer friendly
16

Shriekfest SITE ↗

Los Angeles, CA · October

LA's longest-running horror festival. No-frills, respected, and a legitimate LA laurel for a micro-budget.

traditional horror · scripts too · no-frills respect
The longest-running horror festival in Los Angeles.
17

Another Hole in the Head SITE ↗

San Francisco, CA · December

Bay Area genre institution with a big slate and an open mind. Friendly to experimental horror.

experimental and genre · open-minded · big slate
18

Nevermore Film Festival SITE ↗

Durham, NC · February

The Southeast's veteran horror fest, twenty-five years strong, with a loyal regional audience.

regional institution · loyal audience · 25 years running
19

Knoxville Horror Film Fest SITE ↗

Knoxville, TN · October

Scrappy, beloved, and genuinely fun: grindhouse energy with a real community behind it.

grindhouse and gore · scrappy · fun-first
20

Atlanta Horror Film Festival SITE ↗

Atlanta, GA · Fall

Long-running Southern showcase with a steady indie slate and a growing scene around it.

southern indie showcase · steady slate
21

Etheria Film Night SITE ↗

Los Angeles, CA

The premier showcase for women-directed genre film. If your director is a woman, Etheria belongs at the top of your list, not here at 21.

women-directed · genre shorts · industry attention
22

HorrorHound Film Fest SITE ↗

Cincinnati, OH

Attached to the HorrorHound convention: your film plays to a room that came for horror all weekend.

convention crowd · fan-heavy rooms
23

Buffalo Dreams Fantastic Film Festival SITE ↗

Buffalo, NY · November

Indie-friendly genre fest with a big slate and a reputation for treating small films with respect.

indie-friendly · big slate · respectful to small films
24

A Night of Horror International Film Festival SITE ↗

Eau Claire, WI

The long-running Australian fest, relocated to America: international pedigree, midwestern address.

international pedigree · midwestern home
25

Independent Shorts Awards SITE ↗

Los Angeles, CA · Monthly

An awards competition more than a festival: high volume, monthly winners, annual live screening. Honest take: laurels come easier here, which has its uses early in a film's life. Know what you're buying.

awards competition · monthly · volume laurels
