The best places on earth to show a horror film · ranked · updated September 2026

Made a horror film? This is where it should play. Our editorial ranking of the festivals that matter for independent horror: judged on what they do for FILMMAKERS: discovery, acquisitions, press, audience, and how they treat you when you're nobody yet. The world tier · if you can only submit to five on earth
W1

Sitges Film Festival

Sitges, Spain · October

The Cannes of genre. Fifty-plus years, the biggest fantastic-film festival in the world. A Sitges laurel changes a film's life.

W2

Fantasia International Film Festival

Montreal, Canada · July-August

North America's biggest genre event. Three weeks, deep programming, real acquisitions. The premiere target for serious indie horror on this continent.

W3

FrightFest

London, UK · August

The UK's dark heart. Five days, ferociously loyal audience, and distributors in the seats.

W4

BIFAN (Bucheon International Fantastic Film Festival)

Bucheon, South Korea · June-July

Asia's genre giant, with an industry market attached. If your film travels, it travels here.

W5

Brussels International Fantastic Film Festival (BIFFF)

Brussels, Belgium · Spring

Forty-plus years of beautiful chaos. The rowdiest, most beloved audience in genre cinema.

The United States · ranked top to bottom
1

Fantastic Fest

Austin, TX · September

The biggest genre festival in the US. Industry, press, buyers, and the Alamo Drafthouse machine. The hardest US laurel to get and the most valuable.

2

Screamfest LA

Los Angeles, CA · October · TCL Chinese

The grande dame of American horror-only festivals. Paranormal Activity premiered here. Public director pages for every alum, a YouTube channel that keeps promoting your short for years, and Hollywood in the room.

3

Overlook Film Festival

New Orleans, LA · Spring

Prestige curation in the most haunted city in America. Smaller slate, huge taste; programmers and press treat an Overlook pick as a mark of quality.

4

Beyond Fest

Los Angeles, CA · Fall

The highest-attended genre event in the US. Skews toward big premieres and repertory, so it's a harder door for micro-indies, but the room is unmatched.

5

Nightmares Film Festival

Columbus, OH · October

The filmmaker's festival. One of the best-reviewed events on all of FilmFreeway, a year-round community, and the alumni network that actually answers your emails.

6

Panic Fest

Kansas City, MO · April

Community-driven, press-heavy (Fangoria, Bloody Disgusting cover the lineup), with a virtual edition that extends your reach. Great first big fest for a new filmmaker.

7

Popcorn Frights

Fort Lauderdale, FL · August

The Southeast's biggest horror event: 100+ films a year, aggressive world-premiere slate, and real love for first-timers.

8

Brooklyn Horror Film Festival

Brooklyn, NY · October

New York's horror address. Nitehawk cinemas, sharp curation, Variety coverage, and a slate that mixes buzz titles with true unknowns.

9

Chattanooga Film Festival

Chattanooga, TN · Spring

Genre-soaked and famously warm to filmmakers. The festival other festivals' programmers attend for fun.

10

Telluride Horror Show

Telluride, CO · October

Small, curated, prestigious. Skews toward films that already have heat, but a gorgeous notch in any belt.

11

FilmQuest

Provo, UT · October

Filmmaker-first culture: mixers, prep lists, huge nominee slates. Alumni rave about it the way people rave about summer camp.

12

Salem Horror Fest

Salem, MA · Spring

Horror in the witch city, with a strong indie and queer-horror community and the Romero Foundation fellowship attached.

13

Boston Underground Film Festival

Boston, MA · March

The weird one, in the best way. If your film is strange as well as scary, BUFF's audience was built for it.

14

HollyShorts (Horror Blocks)

Hollywood, CA · August

Oscar-qualifying shorts festival with dedicated horror programming at the TCL. For shorts chasing award eligibility, this is the door.

15

Horrible Imaginings Film Festival

Santa Ana, CA

Nonprofit, panel-heavy, community-first. One of the most welcoming rooms in the genre for a first film.

16

Shriekfest

Los Angeles, CA · October

LA's longest-running horror festival. No-frills, respected, and a legitimate LA laurel for a micro-budget.

17

Another Hole in the Head

San Francisco, CA · December

Bay Area genre institution with a big slate and an open mind. Friendly to experimental horror.

18

Nevermore Film Festival

Durham, NC · February

The Southeast's veteran horror fest, twenty-five years strong, with a loyal regional audience.

19

Knoxville Horror Film Fest

Knoxville, TN · October

Scrappy, beloved, and genuinely fun: grindhouse energy with a real community behind it.

20

Atlanta Horror Film Festival

Atlanta, GA · Fall

Long-running Southern showcase with a steady indie slate and a growing scene around it.

21

Etheria Film Night

Los Angeles, CA

The premier showcase for women-directed genre film. If your director is a woman, Etheria belongs at the top of your list, not here at 21.

22

HorrorHound Film Fest

Cincinnati, OH

Attached to the HorrorHound convention: your film plays to a room that came for horror all weekend.

23

Buffalo Dreams Fantastic Film Festival

Buffalo, NY · November

Indie-friendly genre fest with a big slate and a reputation for treating small films with respect.

24

A Night of Horror International Film Festival

Eau Claire, WI

The long-running Australian fest, relocated to America: international pedigree, midwestern address.

25

Independent Shorts Awards

Los Angeles, CA · Monthly

An awards competition more than a festival: high volume, monthly winners, annual live screening. Honest take: laurels come easier here, which has its uses early in a film's life. Know what you're buying.

Rankings are HorrorMeet's editorial opinion, updated as the circuit changes. Corrections and arguments welcome on the board. Festival directors: come claim your listing.
