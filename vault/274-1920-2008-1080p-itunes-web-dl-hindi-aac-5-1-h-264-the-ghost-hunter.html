1920 is a 2008 Indian Hindi -language supernatural horror film written and directed by Vikram Bhatt . The plot revolves around the events surrounding a married couple living in a haunted house in the year 1920. The film stars debutant actors Rajneesh Duggal and Adah Sharma as the married couple, along with Indraneil Sengupta in a special role.
