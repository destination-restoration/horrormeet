The Girl Who Knew Too Much (1963)

Directed by Mario Bava

The Girl Who Knew Too Much is a 1963 Italian giallo film directed by Mario Bava, featuring a young American woman named Nora who witnesses a murder in Rome but struggles to convince others of what she saw. Nora witnesses a murder while in Rome, but the police do not believe her due to the lack of a body. As she investigates, she becomes embroiled in a series of murders linked to an "Alphabet Killer," who targets victims in alphabetical order. The film explores themes of illusion versus reality, as Nora's credibility is questioned.
