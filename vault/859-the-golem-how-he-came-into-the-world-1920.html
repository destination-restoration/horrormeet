The Golem꞉ How He Came Into The World (1920)

Directed by Paul Wegener, Carl Boese, Henrik Galeen

I've been researching newly public domain films from 1929 and earlier, so I'm uploading the best possible copies that I can find. One of the first great examples of German Expressionism, The Golem: How He Came into the World is a silent horror film that follows a Jewish rabbi as he creates a seemingly indestructible creature to defend his people against discrimination and destruction. Unfortunately, after his people are saved, the Golem turns its violence against his creator.
