REVISED-Shocker Internet Drive In Week 7 REDO - Vampires

Since it was decided that the "Incredible Two Headed Transplant" was copyrighted and my previous version of Week 7 was deleted, I took some advice and created a new presentation. This new feature is a mini vampire festival. It has "Grave of the Vampire" from my original Week 7, but I included a bunch of Hammer movie trailers for various Dracula flicks. So, this is not a double feature, just some trailers, a Bugs Bunny cartoon, some nifty advertisements and "Grave of the Vampire"! For those of you lucky enough to have downloaded the original Week 7, consider yourself blessed; and, the rest...
