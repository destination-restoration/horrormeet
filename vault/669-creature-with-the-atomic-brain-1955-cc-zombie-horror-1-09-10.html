Creature With The Atomic Brain 1955 ★★★ CC: Zombie/Horror [1:09:10]

Directed by Edward L. Cahn

Creature with the Atom Brain is a 1955 American zombie horror science fiction film directed by Edward L. Cahn and starring Richard Denning. It was released as the bottom half of a double feature with It Came from Beneath the Sea. Plot: Several years earlier gangster Frank Buchanan was deported to his native Italy through the efforts of law enforcement authorities and rival gangsters who inform on him.
