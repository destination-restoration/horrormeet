THE SÉANCE

Brent Bailey · The Huntsman · Dead of Night · The Stairs

Sunday, December 13, 7:30 PM, at Landon's place in Laurel Canyon. About ninety minutes at the table.

Brent, here are the questions we have for you. Read them over and send Landon the numbers you love and any you don't want to touch. Nothing is fixed, and we can cut anything afterwards.

The Ouija board is part of the show. A few times we'll ask you a yes-or-no question that you answer only by sliding the planchette, there's a quick lightning round near the end, and at one point you'll spell two letters. We don't share those questions ahead of time. Hold the planchette on your answer for a second so the camera above the board catches it. You get one pass, you can defend one answer after each round, and you can answer out loud any time you'd rather not touch the board.

Anything you are not cleared to talk about yet stays off the table. Tell Landon before we roll and it will not come up.

It's casual. Two guys from a commercial set, a candlelit table, and your stories told your way.

The opening

  • 1You grew up in Tucson and have been a working actor since 2006. What was the first job that made you think this could actually be a living?
  • 2Emma Approved won an Emmy as a web series, years before anyone took the internet seriously. What did playing Alex Knightley teach you about an audience?
  • 3Twenty years in, what do you know now that would have saved you the most time?

The commercial actor

  • 4You have done around 150 commercials. What does a commercial set teach an actor that a film set never will?
  • 5You and Landon met on a Comcast commercial. What do you remember about that day?
  • 6In 2024 you became Target's Santa and the internet lost its mind. What is it like to go viral for a character with no last name?
  • 7Target went a different way this year. How do you hold a part like that when it is not yours to keep?

Into the woods

  • 8When Landon invited you, the first thing you said was "don't forget The Stairs." Why is that one close to your heart?
  • 9The Stairs put you in the woods with a creature. What was that shoot really like, day to day?
  • 10Dante's Hotel was a Tubi Original shot fast. What did you pick up from the horror veterans on that set?
  • 11These films are made quickly and for very little. What does that pressure do to a performance, good and bad?

The Huntsman

  • 12You read The Huntsman for the first time on the plane to Oklahoma City, after another actor dropped out. What did you decide about Reynolds before you landed?
  • 13You have said Reynolds does not think he crosses the line, he believes he is the line. Where did you personally disagree with him?
  • 14You talk about a look doing more than a line. Which silent moment in that film are you proudest of?
  • 15What did you get wrong in your first days on that set, and who helped you fix it?

Dead of Night

  • 16Dead of Night is in theaters right now, a slasher with Dermot Mulroney. Who is Justin Morgan, without spoiling it?
  • 17You have said your scenes were shot apart from the main cast. How do you build fear when the thing you are afraid of is not in the room?

The big rooms and your own work

  • 18You played Ted Sorensen for Rob Reiner in LBJ, across from Woody Harrelson. What did you learn watching Reiner run a set?
  • 19The Idea of You put you in a scene with Anne Hathaway. What do actors at that level do differently between "action" and "cut"?
  • 20You wrote, produced and starred in The Holiday Dating Guide. What did making your own film teach you about the people who hire you?
  • 21You have said you want a great series next. What is the part you have not been offered yet?

Close

  • 22Where should people find you, what should they watch first, and what would you tell an actor who has just been offered their first horror film?
