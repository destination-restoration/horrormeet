THE SÉANCE

Sophia Louisa · Screenwriter, Producer, The Wrecker

Monday, October 19, 7:00 PM, at Landon's place in Laurel Canyon. About ninety minutes at the table.

Sophia, go through these and send back the numbers you love and any you don't want to touch. We won't stick to the list on the night, and anything can be cut afterwards.

Two things so there are no surprises. Near the end there is a short yes/no lightning round: you answer only by sliding the planchette on the Ouija board to YES or NO. You won't see those questions ahead of time, that's the fun. And the board on the table is a game piece, not a ritual.

1. The opening

  • 1In 2014 you were on a show called A Séance with... Tonight you're at a real one. What was that show, and did anything happen?
  • 2You make movies anyway. That's in your bio. What was the moment you decided nobody was going to give you permission?
  • 3What scared you as a kid, and does it still?

2. The Wrecker

  • 4Harvey Keitel, Tyrese Gibson, Mena Suvari and Danny Trejo in one movie. How does a script get that cast, and who said yes first?
  • 5You co-wrote it with Niko Foster and James Dean Simington, and then Paralysis with Niko too. How do you split the work in a writing room of three?
  • 6Shooting in Las Vegas in 2023. What was the hardest day?
  • 7A critic called the script derivative of the action films before it. Was that the point? How do you take a review of something you meant as a throwback?
  • 8What line or scene in The Wrecker is most yours?

3. The horror side

  • 9The Devil's Heist, Witchcraft: The Lanterne of Light, A Soldier's Descent. You keep coming back to horror. What pulls you there?
  • 10The Wailing Room: a room that feeds on grief. Where did that idea come from?
  • 11You acted in The Purging Hour and Psychos before you were producing. What did acting in low-budget horror teach you about writing it?
  • 12What's the most frightening thing that has ever happened to you on a set?
  • 13Witches, demons, a grief-eating room. What's the one horror idea you're scared to write?

4. Making it anyway

  • 1491 credits on IMDb, a lot of them shorts you wrote, directed and acted in. Why keep making shorts when you're producing features?
  • 15What does a producer actually do on a micro-budget horror movie that nobody ever sees?
  • 16Who pays for indie genre films right now, and what do they want to hear in the pitch?
  • 17What were you pitching on Thursday night, if you can say?

5. Sofi Stories, AI and So Zoom In

  • 18Sofi Stories: print, film and AI. Where do you draw the line on AI in screenwriting?
  • 19113 episodes of So Zoom In. What do you ask guests that nobody else asks?
  • 20Who's the guest you most want to get?

6. Close

  • 21What's next, and where should people find you?
  • 22One piece of advice for a horror fan who wants to write their first script.
