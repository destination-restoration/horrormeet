THE SÉANCE

Manta Mystic · Channel, Healer, Spiritual Consultant

A weeknight this week, evening, at Landon's place in Laurel Canyon. About ninety minutes at the table. Day and time: your call.

Manta, tick the ones you want and send back the numbers. Ignore anything you would rather not get into. We do not have to stick to the list on the day, and we can cut anything afterwards.

Two things so there are no surprises. There is a short yes/no round near the end: six questions, you answer only by sliding the planchette to YES or NO, one pass allowed. You will not see those ahead of time, that is the fun. And the live channel is yours to run: you open it, you close it, and you decide what is off limits. The board on the table is our game piece, not a ritual; nothing is called with it.

1. The opening

  • 1Tonight is the first time you have ever channeled on camera. What are you most afraid of going wrong, and what would make it worth it?
  • 2You told Landon you work with an Egyptian goddess named Wadjet. Who is she, how did she find you, and what does she sound like when she comes through?
  • 3You are not a medium. You said you do not talk to lingering spirits or anything of a lower vibration. Draw the line for us: what is the difference between what you do and what the ghost-hunting shows do?

2. How she got here

  • 4What was the first thing that happened to you that you could not explain, and how old were you?
  • 5What were you doing before Bali, and what made you get on the plane?
  • 6Ubud: the School of Unified Healing, Radiantly Alive, and your teacher Bogdan Tummo, fifteen years as a Tibetan monk. What did the first week with him look like, and what was the first thing he told you that you did not believe?
  • 7He initiated you in basically everything you practice and you still work together. What does an initiation actually involve, and what did it change in you?
  • 8You run marathons and half marathons constantly. What does a body built for 26 miles have to do with opening to spirits?

3. The craft

  • 9Walk us through what happens in your body and mind from the moment you sit down to the moment Wadjet is talking.
  • 10You said channeling positive entities of light versus negative ones works differently. How do you know which one has shown up?
  • 11Tibetan protection spells. What are they, who taught them to you, and have they ever been tested?
  • 12You channel spaces and objects too. Can you read this room right now, before we tell you anything about it?
  • 13Kundalini transmission, shaktipat. What does a person feel when you do it to them, and would you do it to Danny?
  • 14What are the practical first steps for someone at home who wants to try channeling and not hurt themselves?

4. The house, and the risk

  • 15There was something in this house once, and a cleansing quieted it. You are going to cleanse the space before we start. What exactly do you do, and what are you looking for?
  • 16Is there a real risk in doing this on camera, in a home, with a Ouija board on the table? What would you refuse to do in someone's house?
  • 17The strangest thing that has ever come through for a client, the one you still think about.
  • 18Has a session ever gone wrong, and what did you do?

5. The business of the unseen

  • 19People pay for channeling sessions, readings and transmissions. How do you price something nobody can verify, and how do you keep yourself honest?
  • 20What do horror movies get right about spirits, and what do they get completely wrong?
  • 21Horror fans are a skeptical crowd. What would you say to the person watching who thinks this is all performance?

6. Close

  • 22Where do people find you and book you, and what should they ask for first?
  • 23What do you want someone to walk away with after watching you channel for the first time?
