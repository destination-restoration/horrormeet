THE SÉANCE

Susan Lanier · Brenda in Wes Craven's The Hills Have Eyes · Actress · Singer

Monday, November 16, 3:00 PM, at Landon's place in Laurel Canyon. About eighty minutes at the table, and you are on the road before the traffic.

Susan, here are the questions we have for you. Read them over and send Landon the numbers you love and any you don't want to touch. Nothing is fixed, and we can cut anything afterwards.

The Ouija board is part of the show. A few times we'll ask you a yes-or-no question that you answer only by sliding the planchette, there's a quick lightning round near the end, and at one point you'll spell two letters. We don't share those questions ahead of time. Hold the planchette on your answer for a second so the camera above the board catches it. You get one pass, you can defend one answer after each round, and you can answer out loud any time you'd rather not touch the board.

If you kept anything from the film, bring it. If a friend is coming with you, tell Landon their name so we can set a chair and a microphone.

It's casual. The point is for you to tell your story, all of it, your way.

The opening

  • 1You grew up in Dallas and landed in Los Angeles in 1974. What did you think you were coming here to be?
  • 2The story goes that on your second day in town you were on the Happy Days set and Henry Winkler asked for you. What happened?
  • 3Pat Hingle taught you a trick on Barnaby Jones for hitting your mark without looking down. What else did the old pros teach you that nobody teaches now?

Getting Brenda

  • 4Your agent told you not to take The Hills Have Eyes. Why did you say yes anyway?
  • 5You met Wes Craven, did some improv, and had the part that day. What do you think he saw in that room?
  • 6Who was Brenda to you before the cameras rolled, and how much of her was already you?

The desert

  • 7Three to four weeks in the Mojave on a grueling low-budget schedule: burning days, freezing nights, one shared trailer. What did a day on that shoot actually look like?
  • 8You came home with real bruises, from the dog and from being pulled by the hair. How far did that shoot push you physically?
  • 9The trailer attack is still one of the hardest scenes in horror to watch. How did you and Michael Berryman get ready for it, and how did you take care of each other?
  • 10Brenda does not stay a victim. By the end she is the bait and the one swinging. Did you fight for that, or was it on the page?
  • 11You have said four or five endings were shot. Which one would you have kept?
  • 12The Hills Have Eyes came out before Halloween. Do you claim the title of first Scream Queen?

After the hills

  • 13You came back from the desert and were paged in a supermarket to screen test for Three's Company that same night. Walk us through those two days.
  • 14Bambi on Welcome Back, Kotter, a series regular on Szysznyk, the Rainbow Hour with Tony Orlando and Dawn. Comedy and horror in the same couple of years. Which one is harder?
  • 15When people tell the story of The Hills Have Eyes, what do they usually leave out?
  • 16You were at the 2002 Santa Monica screening with Wes when a fan asked about a remake. Do you think that question started it, and what should happen when the film turns fifty next year?

The music and the camera

  • 17A musician saw the film at a drive-in and told you the next night that he loved the way you scream. What happened after that?
  • 18The Palomino Club, Swamp Cabaret, Allergic to Texas, and a new single you are recording in Palm Springs. What does singing give you that acting never did?
  • 19You picked up a camera in 1984 and were shooting headshots within weeks. What do you see in an actor's face that they cannot see themselves?

Horror now

  • 20You do not watch horror movies alone, and you love Vincent Price. What should a horror film have that most of them forget?
  • 21Lately you play the villains: a senator in Red Rooms, a killer bartender in The Boatyard, and Heidi in The Black Mass for Devanny Pinn. Is it more fun on that side?

Close

  • 22What is next, where should people find you and the new single, and what would you tell a young actress who has just been offered her first horror film?
