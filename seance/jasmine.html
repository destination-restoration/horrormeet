THE SÉANCE

Jasmine Davis · Killer Revenge

Saturday October 4, 2026, 4:00 PM. About thirty minutes. Phone or video, wherever you are.

Jasmine, tick the ones you want and send it back. Ignore anything you would rather not get into.

We do not have to stick to the list on the day, and we can cut anything afterwards.

There are no wrong answers here. The premise of the whole sitting is that you finished a feature,

which almost nobody who starts one ever does.

1. The opening

  • 1Killer Revenge is out this week. How does it feel to have people finally able to press play?
  • 2For someone who has not seen it yet, what is it about, in your own words?
  • 3You wrote it, directed it, cut it, scored it and acted in it. Which of those jobs did you enjoy most, and which one would you hand to somebody else next time?
  • 4When did you know you were actually going to finish it, rather than hoping?

2. How it got made

  • 5IMDb lists the budget at seven thousand dollars. Is that about right, and what does seven thousand dollars actually buy you?
  • 6Eighteen people in the cast on that budget. How do you get eighteen actors to show up?
  • 7What did you shoot it on, and why that?
  • 8What did people tell you that you could not do with the gear you had, that you went and did anyway?
  • 9How big was the crew on your biggest day?
  • 10What is the single thing you could not afford that you found a way around, and how did you do it?
  • 11How long was the shoot in total, and how spread out?
  • 12Was there a day where you thought it was over? What kept it alive?

3. Directing for the first time

  • 13Nobody teaches you how to give a note. How did you work out how to talk to your actors?
  • 14You were directing and acting in the same scenes. How did you know your own performance was landing with nobody at the monitor?
  • 15What surprised you most about directing that nothing had prepared you for?
  • 16Was there a moment where an actor gave you something better than what you wrote?
  • 17Did you storyboard, shot list, or work it out on the day?

4. The edit

  • 18Cutting your own film means killing your own work. What was the hardest thing you cut?
  • 19Did the film get shorter or longer than you planned in the edit, and what did that teach you?
  • 20At what point did you stop being the writer and start being the editor?
  • 21Is there a scene that only works because of something that happened in post?

5. Putting it out yourself

  • 22You are releasing on YouTube rather than waiting on a distributor. Talk us through that decision.
  • 23What is the upside of self-releasing that filmmakers underrate?
  • 24What would have to be true for you to go the distributor route on the next one?

6. Killer Revenge 2

  • 25You are already writing the second one. What is it about?
  • 26What is the single biggest thing you are doing differently?
  • 27What are you keeping exactly the same because it worked?
  • 28Is there a thing from the first one you now want to go back and fix in the sequel?
  • 29Bigger budget, or same budget spent smarter? Where does the money go first?

7. For everyone about to make their first one

  • 30What is the piece of advice you would give someone on day one of their first feature?
  • 31What is the pitfall you walked straight into that you can now see coming a mile off?
  • 32What did you spend money on that was worth every cent, and what was not?
  • 33What do people get wrong about how hard the first one is?
  • 34If you had to start over tomorrow with the same money, what is the first thing you would do differently?

8. The fun end

  • 35If someone handed you ten times the budget tomorrow, what is the first thing you would spend it on?
  • 36Which horror actor would you most want to direct, and what would you put them through?
  • 37What horror film made you want to make horror films?
  • 38What is the most underrated horror film nobody talks about?
  • 39Practical effects or digital, and why?
  • 40What scares you personally, that you have not put on screen yet?

9. Close

  • 41Where should people watch Killer Revenge, and where do they follow you for the second one?
  • 42What do you want people to take away from it?
