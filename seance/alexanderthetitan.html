THE SÉANCE

Alexanderthetitan: Alex + Adam · Short horror films · 243K on YouTube · VCR Willie

Saturday, October 24, 7:00 PM, at Landon's place in Laurel Canyon. About ninety minutes at the table.

Here are the questions we have for the two of you. Read them over and send Landon the numbers you love and any you don't want to touch. Nothing is fixed, and we can cut anything afterwards.

Near the end there is a quick yes-or-no lightning round on the Ouija board. We don't share those ahead of time, and you can answer out loud if you'd rather not touch the board.

It's casual. The point is for you to tell your story.

The opening

  • 1Alex, 243,000 people subscribe to a channel called Alexanderthetitan. Where did the name come from?
  • 2How did you two meet, and how did Adam end up at this table tonight?
  • 3What scared each of you as a kid, and does it still?

The viral years

  • 4Mama Agnes is under three minutes and has over 11 million views. Did you know it was the one when you cut it?
  • 5Santa in 2019 and The Red Nightmare in 2021 both passed 7 million. What did those early hits teach you about the first ten seconds of a short?
  • 6Skibidi Toilet, Barbie, the Grimace Shake, Hawk Tuah. You turned internet memes into horror within days. How fast does one go from idea to upload?
  • 7Aunty Mabel became a franchise: three films and Unblooded. When did you know people would follow her?
  • 8"Read the book for the ending." Why put the ending of Mama Agnes in a book?

Making it

  • 9You shot Doppelgänger on a used $300 pocket camera with no bag, in about two hours. What did that teach you?
  • 10The same names keep showing up in your credits for music, sound, VFX and casting. How did you find a crew that stays?
  • 11Adam, what is your job on a shoot day, and what is the hardest part of it?
  • 12Casting a short that has to land in three minutes: what are you looking for in the room?
  • 13What is the scariest thing that has happened on one of your sets?
  • 14Trains, apartments, streets. How do you shoot in public without the world noticing?

VCR Willie

  • 15VCR Willie, Tape 1 and 2 and Murder Boy run over an hour each, and the VHS, DVD and flash drives sold out. Why go physical in 2026?
  • 16What does tape do to fear that 4K can't?
  • 17THREATS, GOREVERITE, OPPS, TROWEL. One-word titles in capital letters. Is this a new era for the channel?

The business

  • 18The algorithm for horror shorts has changed since 2023. What changed, and what are you doing about it?
  • 19Rain Of Titans. What is the plan, and what would it take to make a feature?
  • 20Who made you want to do this? Drew Daywalt is credited on Doppelgänger. Who else?

Close

  • 21What is next, and where should people find you?
  • 22One piece of advice for a kid with a phone who wants to make a first horror short.
