THE SÉANCE

Patti Negri · The Good Witch of Hollywood · Psychic Medium · Ghost Adventures

Monday, December 14, in the evening, at Landon's place in Laurel Canyon. You pick the start time. About ninety minutes at the table.

Patti, thank you for doing this. Here is what the night is: you, Landon and Danny at a candlelit table, cameras rolling, talking for about ninety minutes. It comes out as an episode of The Séance on YouTube, Spotify and Apple about a week later.

Is it a real séance? It is a conversation first. But there is a Ouija board on the table every episode, and with you here we want to do it properly. You offered to lift the veil of the house, and we would love to open the show with that. We also want you to teach us the board: what we have been doing wrong, your rules, and how to close it. If you will write us a close, we will use it to end every episode from now on.

What to bring: yourself, and anything you like to have on a table when you work (candles, a crystal, a doll, tools). Come a little early and walk the house first. If you feel something and want to do a cleansing, tell us and we will ask before we film any of it.

Below are the questions we would love to ask. Send Landon the numbers you love and any you do not want to touch, and tell us what we got wrong. There is also a quick yes or no lightning round near the end that we keep as a surprise. Nothing is fixed, and anything can be cut afterwards.

The good witch

  • 1You held your first séance at about seven. Who was at the table, and who showed up?
  • 2You have said your mother and grandmother both had the gift. What did they teach you that you still use every week?
  • 3You worked in Hollywood for decades before you "came out of the broom closet" in 2008. Why then, and what did it cost you?
  • 4What does "good witch" actually mean in practice? Where is your line?

Hollywood

  • 5Take us to the set of Rocky III. How did you land it, and is it true that is where you got your SAG card?
  • 6I Play Rocky is in theaters right now, the story of Stallone fighting to make the first one. You were there for the third. What was he like on set?
  • 7Miami Vice, Hitchcock Presents, Days of Our Lives. What did the 80s teach you about surviving as a working actor?
  • 8In Portraits you play Maxine Sanders, a real witch. What do films get wrong about witches every single time?

Board school

  • 9We put a Ouija board on this table every episode. Honestly: have we been doing it wrong?
  • 10Our director thinks we may be inviting in things we should not. Is he right? How would we know?
  • 11What are your rules before anyone touches a board? Walk us through them like we are seven.
  • 12You say most of what people call demons is "just a cranky ghost." How do you tell the difference?
  • 13How do you close a board properly, and will you write us a close we can use to end every episode?

The scary ones

  • 14You call Sowden House, the Black Dahlia house, your scariest site. What happened in that séance?
  • 15The Cecil Hotel: you called it the darkest place you have ever been. What did you see there?
  • 16You told me you did three séances in one night at Pennhurst Asylum. What does that take out of you?
  • 17On Ghost Adventures you held a séance for Houdini, the man who spent his life exposing mediums. Did he come through?

The happy ones

  • 18Not every spirit story is scary. What is the happiest thing that has ever come through at one of your tables?
  • 19You helped move Aaron Spelling on from his own mansion. How do you talk someone into leaving?

This house

  • 20You walked this house before we rolled. What did you feel, and where?
  • 21Does it need anything? A cleansing, a rule, a room we should stay out of?

Close

  • 22Where can people find you, your books and your classes, and what is the one thing you want every person with a board in their closet to know?
