THE SÉANCE

Jacob + Sam Harding · Paralysis · Withdrawal · The Wailing Man

Sunday, November 29, 7:30 PM, at Landon's place in Laurel Canyon. About ninety minutes at the table.

Jacob and Sam, here are the questions we have for you. Read them over and send Landon the numbers you love and any you don't want to touch. Nothing is fixed, and we can cut anything afterwards.

No Ouija board at this sitting. It will not be on the table or anywhere in the room. A few times we'll ask a quick yes-or-no question that you both answer out loud at the same time, and there's a fast lightning round near the end. We don't share those questions ahead of time. You get one pass, and you can defend one answer after each round.

Anything you are not cleared to talk about yet, like distribution or release dates, stays off the table. Tell Landon before we roll and it will not come up.

It's casual. Two brothers, a candlelit table, a demon, and the real story of how Paralysis got made.

Two brothers from Kansas City

  • 1Jacob, you started at 12 with an iPod Touch and a Batman parody called Fatman. What did that first movie teach you?
  • 2Sam, you were in Annie at 8. When did acting stop being a hobby and become the plan?
  • 3You moved to LA together in 2020. What did the first year look like, and what almost sent you home?

The film school you make yourself

  • 4XYZ was a 71-minute feature with more than fifty people on it. Who paid for it, and what went wrong?
  • 5Withdrawal is a hard film about addiction. Why make that story, and what did it teach you about directing your brother?

The demon is real

  • 7Sam, you have had sleep paralysis yourself. What did it feel like, and which scene in the film comes straight from it?
  • 8How do you design a demon that has to be scary for ninety minutes on an indie budget?
  • 9Why set it in 1978? What does that year give a horror film that today doesn't?
  • 10Sam, Robbie carries his family's drowning. How did you get there, night after night, and how did you leave it at the end of the day?

Making Paralysis

  • 11How does a first feature land Dee Wallace? Walk us through the ask.
  • 12What did Dee bring on the day that wasn't on the page?
  • 13Shooting a period film in Missouri: cars, wardrobe, locations. What did you have to cut?
  • 14Peter Foldy introduced us. How did he and the rest of your producers come aboard?
  • 15Jacob, you say one bad moment can derail a day. What was the worst day on the Paralysis set?

Brothers on set

  • 16When the director and the lead are brothers, who wins the argument?
  • 17Sam, you are also a producer on it. Did wearing both hats ever get in the way of the performance?
  • 18Jacob directed your music videos, and Cry won at Valley Film Fest. Does the music side feed the films?

Getting it seen

  • 19There are at least three films called Paralysis. How do you make sure people find yours?
  • 20What is the festival and release plan, and what would a win look like for you a year from now?
  • 21Sam, you played a killer for Lifetime. What did TV movies teach you that you brought to your own film?

Close

  • 22Where can people follow Paralysis and find you two, and what would you tell two siblings trying to make their first feature together?
