Konrad Parker Presents: Friday the 13th, The Culver Theater · November 13, 2026

The Jason X 25th anniversary panel

On stage: Derwin Jordan (Waylander) and Adam Marcus (director, Jason Goes to Hell) on stage; Lexa Doig (Rowan) by video

Hosted by Konrad Parker

· Watch on YouTube ↗

What was said

The panel from Konrad Parker's Jason X 25th anniversary night at The Culver Theater in Culver City, Friday the 13th, November 13, 2026. Derwin Jordan (Waylander) and Jason Goes to Hell director Adam Marcus were on stage, and final girl Lexa Doig (Rowan) joined by video. Landon Brittain of HorrorMeet filmed it from the audience; the recording picks up partway through the Q&A.

Lexa Doig says the production never asked her to explain her ethnicity, and the panel talks about what it meant that Jason X let two Black heroes go out fighting for other people and a Filipina final girl survive on her smarts. That leads to the story of Nichelle Nichols, Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. and Mae Jemison.

Asked what they would say to the haters 25 years on, the answers range from "an opinion is not a fact" to "it sucks to be them." Adam Marcus tells how he brought cinematographer Bill Dill onto Jason Goes to Hell, and the story of Kane Hodder and Bill's young son Elliot on the last day of the shoot. Lexa Doig closes with watching Jason X with Lisa Ryder while they were filming Andromeda.
