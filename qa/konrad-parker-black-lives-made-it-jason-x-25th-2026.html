Konrad Parker Presents: Friday the 13th, The Culver Theater · November 13, 2026

Konrad Parker on Black Lives Made It

On stage: Konrad Parker (host and programmer)

Hosted by Landon Brittain and Danny Alamary

· Watch on YouTube ↗

What was said

Filmed in the lobby of The Culver Theater on Friday the 13th, November 13, 2026, before Konrad Parker's 25th anniversary screening of Jason X. The night brought Derwin Jordan (Waylander) for his first public appearance, Jason Goes to Hell director Adam Marcus in person, and Lexa Doig, the film's final girl, by video. Tickets were $12.29.

Parker says the series started because he was tired of watching Black characters die first. Black Lives Made It celebrates the horror films where the Black character survives to the end, or at least is the last one to go. He runs about one night a month at The Culver Theater and another at 33 Taps down the street, and pays for it himself.

Next at The Culver: the 15th anniversary of Scream 4 on Saturday, December 5, 2026, with producer Marianne Maddalena.
