Los Angeles premiere, Landmark Sunset · September 24, 2026

Hellbilly Hollow cast and crew Q&A

On stage: Kevin Wayne (director, plays Tickles) and Kurt Deimer (plays Bull, co-producer)

Hosted by Victoria Venin

· Watch on YouTube ↗

What was said

Filmed at the Los Angeles premiere of Hellbilly Hollow at the Landmark Sunset on September 24, 2026, and hosted by Victoria Venin. Kevin Wayne directed the film and plays Tickles; Kurt Deimer plays Bull and co-produced.

The film was shot at a real haunted attraction in Alabama, and the three men who own that attraction are executive producers. Wayne and Deimer each lived in an RV on the property during the shoot. Deimer improvised most of his dialogue while Wayne directed the scenes as the silent brother standing beside him. Hellbilly Hollow premiered at the New York City Horror Film Festival in December 2022 and reached theaters in September 2026.
