Derwin Jordan at the Jason X 25th anniversary

On stage: Derwin Jordan (Waylander)

Hosted by Landon Brittain and Danny Alamary

· Watch on YouTube ↗

What was said

Filmed in the lobby of The Culver Theater on Friday the 13th, November 13, 2026, at Konrad Parker's Jason X 25th anniversary night. It was Derwin Jordan's first public appearance.

Jordan auditioned in 1999 for a different part. Before he read, he asked director Jim Isaac and the Cunninghams whether the character dies, and when. The answer was page 20. He joked that he could not audition for it, then did, and two weeks later his agent called to say he had booked Jason X in another role. He read the script and found that Waylander gets almost all the way to the end and dies a hero. On his first day on set, Isaac came up to him and said, "See? We took care of you."

The film was shot in 2000, entirely on studio sets in Canada, over two and a half months, with two weeks of rehearsal first. Jordan says the cast rehearsed in an empty warehouse and were blown away when they finally saw the build, with only small pieces left to CGI. He had done science fiction before, including 22 episodes of NightMan and an episode of Mutant X, which helped when acting to things that were not there.

He also talks about why auditioning has been harder since COVID, now that everything is a self-tape and there is no room to read, and about growing up watching the Friday the 13th and Halloween films every Friday the 13th.
