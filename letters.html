Open your letterbox

  • No harassment, threats, sexual messages to anyone who did not ask for them, or anything involving minors. Ever.
  • No spam, no selling outside the Estate Sale, no asking for money.
  • First letters cannot contain links. Text only, no pictures.
  • Reported conversations are read by the HorrorMeet moderators. Nothing else is.
