THE SÉANCE
Live Q&As with the people who make horror

Once in a while, we sit somebody down at the table: a director, an effects artist, a scream queen, a legend: and the whole community gets to ask. Questions gather on the board ahead of time; the guest answers live in their own thread. No moderators reading softballs off cards. Your questions, their answers, on the record forever.

🕯
How a sitting works: the guest is announced on the board · members drop questions in the summoning thread · at the appointed hour, the guest joins and answers live · the thread stays up forever as part of the record.
First sittings are being scheduled now. We're starting with working horror filmmakers and building toward names you grew up scared of. Watch the board for the first summoning.
Made horror? Sit with us. Directors, producers, FX artists, actors: if your work is on the film shelf, you can host a sitting. Message @LandonXXL on the board.

Guests speak for themselves. The circle is respectful or the circle is closed. House rules apply.
